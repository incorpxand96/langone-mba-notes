# Accrual vs. Cash

## Definition

Accrual accounting recognizes economic activity when value is created or consumed rather than only when cash moves.

## Four Timing Patterns

### Revenue Before Receipt

Creates receivable.

### Receipt Before Revenue

Creates deferred revenue.

### Expense Before Payment

Creates payable/accrued expense.

### Payment Before Expense

Creates prepaid/deferred expense.

## Balance-Sheet Interpretation

Many operating assets and liabilities are accumulated timing differences.

## Principle

> **Profit and cash answer different questions.**

The gap between them is often economically informative rather than an accounting inconvenience.
