# Capitalization vs. Expensing

## Core Question

Does the cost still produce a measurable future benefit?

## Expense

Recognize cost now when the resource has been consumed.

## Capitalize

Record an asset when the resource is expected to create future benefits.

Then recognize the cost later through:

- COGS;
- depreciation;
- amortization.

## Examples

- inventory → COGS when sold;
- PP&E → depreciation as used;
- prepaid services → expense over use period.

## Principle

> **Capitalizing a cost delays expense recognition; it does not make the cost disappear.**

## Analytical Risk

Different capitalization policies can affect:

- current profit;
- asset balances;
- future expense;
- cash-flow presentation.

Cash economics must therefore be analyzed separately from accounting timing.
