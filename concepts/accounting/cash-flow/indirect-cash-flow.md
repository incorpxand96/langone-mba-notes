# Indirect Cash Flow

## Purpose

The indirect method explains why:

> **net income ≠ operating cash flow**

## Starting Point

Begin with net income.

Then remove:

- non-cash expenses;
- non-operating gains/losses;
- operating timing differences.

## Working-Capital Logic

Increases in operating assets generally reduce cash relative to earnings.

Increases in operating liabilities generally increase cash relative to earnings.

## Depreciation

Depreciation is added back because:

- it lowered net income;
- it did not cause a current operating cash outflow.

## Earnings Quality

Large or persistent differences between:

- income;
- cash generation

deserve investigation.

## Principle

> **The indirect cash flow statement is a reconciliation from accounting performance to cash consequences.**
