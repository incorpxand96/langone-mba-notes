# Working Capital and Funding Needs

## Definition

Operating working capital:

`Current Operating Assets − Current Operating Liabilities`

It measures short-cycle operating capital tied up between payments and receipts.

## Increases Funding Need

- receivables;
- inventory;
- prepayments.

## Reduces Funding Need

- payables;
- accrued expenses;
- deferred revenue.

## Growth Effect

Growth often consumes cash because operating assets must rise before collections arrive.

## Optimization

Lower working capital is not automatically better.

Examples:

- less inventory can create stockouts;
- tighter customer terms can reduce sales;
- stretching suppliers can raise purchase price.

## Principle

> **Optimize working capital economically; do not minimize it mechanically.**
