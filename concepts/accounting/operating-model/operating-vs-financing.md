# Operating vs. Financing

## Why Separate Them?

Operating items tell us how the enterprise performs.

Financial items tell us how that enterprise is funded and how liquidity is held.

## Operating

Examples:

- receivables;
- inventory;
- PP&E;
- payables;
- deferred revenue;
- EBIT.

## Financial

Examples:

- debt;
- excess cash;
- financial investments;
- interest income;
- interest expense.

## Net Operating Assets

`NOA = Operating Assets − Operating Liabilities`

## Invested Capital

`IC = Equity + Financial Liabilities − Financial Assets`

Conceptually:

`NOA = IC`

## Principle

> **Analyze the business first, then analyze how it is financed.**

This avoids giving financing choices credit for operating quality.
