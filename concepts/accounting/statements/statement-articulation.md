# Financial Statement Articulation

## Definition

Financial statements articulate when the stock and flow measures reconcile through accounting identities.

## Core Links

`Beginning Cash + Change in Cash = Ending Cash`

`Beginning Equity + Change in Equity = Ending Equity`

`Beginning Retained Earnings + Net Income − Dividends = Ending Retained Earnings`

## Mental Model

- balance sheet → what remains at a point in time;
- income statement → changes due to income-generating activity;
- cash flow statement → changes in cash;
- equity statement → changes in owners' residual claim.

## Principle

> **Never analyze one financial statement as though the others do not exist.**

An income statement trend often creates:

- a balance-sheet consequence;
- a cash-flow consequence.
