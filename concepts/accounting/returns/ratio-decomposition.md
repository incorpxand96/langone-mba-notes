# Ratio Decomposition

## Core Operating Decomposition

`Pre-Tax ROIC = Operating Margin × Invested Capital Turnover`

where:

`Operating Margin = EBIT / Sales`

`Invested Capital Turnover = Sales / Invested Capital`

This separates:

- profitability per unit of revenue;
- revenue generated per unit of capital.

## Drill-Down Ratios

Turnover can be decomposed through:

- receivables;
- inventory;
- payables;
- PP&E.

Margins can be decomposed through:

- gross margin;
- operating expense ratios.

## Leverage

ROE then adds:

- leverage benefit;
- financing cost;
- taxes.

## Valuation

Market ratios add:

- payout;
- growth;
- required return.

## Principle

> **Ratio analysis is most useful when it explains cause-and-effect rather than merely ranking numbers.**
