# Bullwhip Effect

## Definition

Small end-demand changes become progressively larger upstream order swings.

## Drivers

1. forecast updating;
2. order batching;
3. price fluctuations;
4. rationing / shortage gaming.

## Consequences

- excess inventory;
- stockouts;
- unstable capacity;
- higher transportation cost;
- poor service.

## Mitigation

- share actual demand;
- shorten lead times;
- place smaller/frequent orders;
- stabilize prices;
- design allocation rules that do not reward inflated ordering.

## Principle

> **Local optimization can destabilize the system when actors respond to distorted information rather than true demand.**
