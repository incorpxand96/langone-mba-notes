# Cash Conversion Cycle

## Formula

`CCC = DIO + DSO − DPO`

## Little's Law Interpretation

`DIO = Inventory / COGS per day`

`DSO = Receivables / Sales per day`

`DPO = Payables / Purchases per day`

## Economic Meaning

CCC measures how long the firm finances the operating cycle before recovering cash.

## Operational Levers

- inventory flow;
- production lead time;
- sell-through;
- customer collection;
- supplier terms.

## Cross-Course Link

Accounting shows the balances.

Operations explains the process and timing.

Finance values the resulting cash-flow consequences.

## Principle

> **Working capital is flow time expressed in dollars.**
