# Strategic Operational Fit

## Definition

Strategic operational fit exists when an operating system's capabilities reinforce the product/customer strategy.

## Product Dimensions

Useful dimensions:

- cost;
- variety;
- response time;
- quality.

## Operating Capabilities

Capabilities arise from:

- resources;
- process design;
- information;
- technology;
- supply chain;
- culture.

## Diagnostic Sequence

1. Define target customer and product promise.
2. Identify operational objectives.
3. Map resources/processes.
4. Test fit.
5. Trace capabilities into economics.

## Principle

> **Operational excellence means being excellent at the capabilities the strategy actually requires—not maximizing every operational dimension.**
