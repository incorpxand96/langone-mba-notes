# Little's Law

## Formula

`I = R × T`

where:

- `I` = inventory / units in process;
- `R` = throughput;
- `T` = flow time.

## Why It Is Powerful

Little's Law connects:

- quantity;
- rate;
- time.

It applies to:

- customers;
- claims;
- orders;
- physical inventory;
- receivables;
- payables;
- queues.

## Analytical Steps

1. Define system boundary.
2. Define flow unit.
3. Make units consistent.
4. Identify two variables.
5. Solve for the third.

## Principle

> **If something flows through a stable system, its amount in the system is often interpretable as throughput multiplied by time.**
