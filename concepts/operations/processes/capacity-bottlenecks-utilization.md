# Capacity, Bottlenecks, and Utilization

## Capacity

Maximum sustainable flow rate.

## Utilization

`Utilization = Throughput / Capacity`

## Bottleneck

The binding resource that limits total system throughput.

For a simple serial process:

`System Capacity = min(Stage Capacities)`

## Investment Rule

Adding capacity to a non-bottleneck typically does not raise system output.

## Queue Insight

Queues can propagate upstream.

The visible queue can therefore be far from the true bottleneck.

## Principle

> **Improve the constraint, not merely the congestion.**
