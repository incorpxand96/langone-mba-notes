# Constraints and Shadow Prices

## Binding Constraint

A resource fully consumed at the optimal solution.

## Non-Binding Constraint

A resource with slack remaining.

## Shadow Price

The marginal improvement in the objective from one additional unit of a binding resource, within the sensitivity range.

## Economic Interpretation

A shadow price answers:

> **What is one more unit of this bottleneck worth?**

This sets an upper bound on how much it is economically sensible to pay for incremental capacity, before considering broader effects.

## Caveat

Sensitivity results are local.

They apply only:

- within allowable ranges;
- generally one parameter change at a time.

## Principle

> **The value of capacity depends on whether it is constraining the objective.**
