# Time Value of Money

## Definition

Money received at different dates is not economically equivalent.

A dollar today can be invested and earn a return.

## Core Relationships

`FV = PV(1+r)^T`

`PV = FV/(1+r)^T`

## Why It Matters

TVM turns timing into value.

It is the foundation of:

- bond pricing;
- DCF;
- loan amortization;
- capital budgeting;
- retirement saving;
- option strike discounting.

## Principle

> **Do not compare cash flows at different dates without first moving them to a common date.**
