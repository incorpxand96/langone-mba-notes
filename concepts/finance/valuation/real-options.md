# Real Options

Real-options thinking values managerial flexibility in uncertain investments.

Potential options include:

- expand;
- defer;
- abandon;
- redeploy;
- stage investment.

## DCF Relationship

Real options are a complement to DCF, not a substitute.

DCF captures expected cash flows; option thinking captures the value of changing course as information arrives.

## Principle

> **Uncertainty creates option value only when management actually has valuable choices after uncertainty resolves.**
