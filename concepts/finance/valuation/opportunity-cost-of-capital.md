# Opportunity Cost of Capital

## Definition

The opportunity cost of capital is the return available from the best comparable-risk alternative.

It is the economically relevant discount rate.

## Valuation Effect

Holding cash flows constant:

- required return ↑ → value ↓;
- required return ↓ → value ↑.

## Risk Matching

The alternative must have similar risk.

Do not discount:

- risky equity cash flows;
- guaranteed Treasury cash flows

at the same rate merely because both occur in the future.

## Cross-Course Use

This concept links:

- accounting cash-flow forecasts;
- business-driver assumptions;
- finance valuation.

## Rule

> **The discount rate is a market opportunity cost, not a management preference.**
