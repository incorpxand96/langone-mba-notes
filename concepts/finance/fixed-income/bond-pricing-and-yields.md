# Bond Pricing and Yields

## Bond as Cash-Flow Package

A coupon bond is a set of dated cash flows.

Each can be valued as a zero-coupon payoff.

## Price

`P = Σ CFt/(1+rt)^t`

## YTM

Yield to maturity is the single discount rate that reproduces the observed price.

It is a summary measure, not a guarantee of realized return.

## Price–Yield Relationship

Fixed cash flows become less valuable when market required returns rise.

Therefore:

> **bond price and yield move inversely.**

## Realized Return

Realized return can differ from initial YTM because of:

- reinvestment;
- early sale;
- yield changes;
- credit events.
