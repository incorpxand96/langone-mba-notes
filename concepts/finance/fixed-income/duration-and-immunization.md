# Duration and Immunization

## Duration

Duration measures:

- weighted-average cash-flow timing;
- local sensitivity to yield changes.

Approximation:

`ΔP/P ≈ −Modified Duration × Δy`

## Drivers

Duration tends to increase with:

- maturity.

Duration tends to decrease with:

- coupon rate;
- yield.

## Asset-Liability Management

If assets and liabilities have different duration, interest-rate changes can alter equity value dramatically.

## Immunization

Match:

- asset PV to liability PV;
- asset duration to liability duration.

This reduces exposure to small parallel yield shifts.

## Limitations

Immunization:

- requires rebalancing;
- is approximate;
- does not fully protect against nonparallel curve shifts.
