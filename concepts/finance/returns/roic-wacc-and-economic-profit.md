# ROIC, WACC, and Economic Profit

## ROIC

`ROIC = NOPAT / Invested Capital`

## Economic Hurdle

Compare ROIC with the opportunity cost of capital:

- `ROIC > WACC` → positive economic profit;
- `ROIC = WACC` → competitive return;
- `ROIC < WACC` → value destruction.

## Economic Profit

`EP = (ROIC - WACC) × Invested Capital`

## Strategic Use

ROIC is evidence that can help validate a strategy narrative, but high ROIC does not identify the source of advantage by itself.

## Principle

> **Explain the operating cause of excess returns rather than treating the ratio as the strategy.**
