# CAPM and Beta

## Beta

`βi = Cov(Ri,RM)/Var(RM)`

Beta measures sensitivity to systematic market risk.

## CAPM

`E(Ri) = Rf + βi[E(RM) − Rf]`

## Interpretation

Required return equals:

- time value of money;
- plus compensation for non-diversifiable risk.

## Corporate Use

CAPM can estimate a required equity return or project hurdle rate when risk is appropriately matched.

That creates a bridge from markets to NPV.

## Caveat

CAPM is a model built on restrictive assumptions.

Its enduring value is often conceptual:

> **risk should be priced according to contribution to diversified portfolio risk, not standalone variance.**
