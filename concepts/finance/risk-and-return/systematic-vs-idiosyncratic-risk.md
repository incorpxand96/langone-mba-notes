# Systematic vs. Idiosyncratic Risk

## Systematic Risk

Risk shared with the broader market/economy.

Cannot be eliminated by broad diversification.

## Idiosyncratic Risk

Risk specific to a company or security.

Can be reduced through diversification.

## Pricing Implication

CAPM says investors should be compensated for systematic risk, not diversifiable idiosyncratic risk.

## Principle

> **Standalone volatility is not the same thing as priced risk.**
