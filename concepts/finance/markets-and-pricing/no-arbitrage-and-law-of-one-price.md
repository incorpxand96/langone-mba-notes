# No Arbitrage and Law of One Price

## Arbitrage

A pure arbitrage requires:

- no net initial investment;
- no negative future cash flow;
- positive payoff in at least one state.

## Law of One Price

If two securities produce identical payoffs, they must have the same price.

## Replication

If a portfolio reproduces a security's payoff, then:

> **security price = cost of replicating portfolio**

## Applications

This principle drives:

- bond pricing;
- forward rates;
- synthetic securities;
- put-call parity;
- binomial option valuation.

## Rule

> **When uncertain how to price a security, first ask whether its cash flows can be replicated.**
