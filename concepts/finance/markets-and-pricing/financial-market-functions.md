# Financial Market Functions

Financial markets help:

1. shift consumption through time;
2. allocate capital;
3. allocate risk.

They connect:

- issuers;
- investors;
- intermediaries;
- regulators.

## Core Market Tasks

Markets must enable participants to:

- search;
- transact;
- enforce.

## Principle

> **Finance is not only valuation mathematics; it is also institutional design for moving capital and risk.**
