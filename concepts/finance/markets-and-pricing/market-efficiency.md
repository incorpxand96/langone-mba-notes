# Market Efficiency

## Definition

A market is informationally efficient when prices rapidly incorporate relevant available information.

## Forms

- weak;
- semi-strong;
- strong.

## What Efficiency Does Not Mean

It does not mean:

- prices never deviate from fundamental value;
- nobody ever outperforms;
- prices always rise on good news.

The relevant issue is whether abnormal risk-adjusted profits can be earned systematically from the information set.

## Practical Standard

Any claimed anomaly should survive adjustments for:

- risk;
- transaction costs;
- taxes;
- liquidity;
- implementation;
- data mining.
