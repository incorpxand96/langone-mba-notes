# Q&A

## Definition

Q&A is the part of a communication where audience concerns, objections, uncertainty, and curiosity become visible.

The goal is to respond credibly while maintaining message discipline.

---

## Core Moves

### Listen

Do not answer the question you expected instead of the question asked.

### Paraphrase

Useful when:

- question is long;
- question is unclear;
- whole audience needs context.

### Answer Briefly

Avoid turning every answer into a second presentation.

### Bridge

Return to the relevant Big Idea.

### Reframe

Useful for:

- false choices;
- loaded language;
- overly broad questions.

### Admit Uncertainty

If I do not know, say so.

Offer a follow-up.

---

## Question Patterns

Common patterns:

- global;
- non-question;
- hypothetical;
- forced choice;
- empty-chair;
- loaded/leading;
- unknown answer.

Recognizing the pattern reduces surprise.

---

## How I Think About It

Q&A is a test of strategic understanding.

If I know:

- the audience;
- the intent;
- the message;

I can improvise without losing coherence.

---

## Rules of Thumb

- Anticipate questions.
- Listen completely.
- Reframe bad premises.
- State assumptions when speculating.
- Do not speak for absent authority.
- Neutralize loaded language.
- Do not bluff.
- Bridge back to the message.
- Keep the whole room involved.
