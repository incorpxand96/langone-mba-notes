# Impromptu Speaking

## Definition

Impromptu speaking is structured thinking under time pressure.

It is not stream of consciousness.

---

## Prepare for Spontaneity

Before high-stakes situations, anticipate:

- context;
- likely topics;
- likely objections;
- important points.

---

## In the Moment

1. Pause.
2. Define the point.
3. Choose two or three supporting ideas.
4. Speak conversationally.
5. End clearly.

---

## How I Think About It

The ability to improvise is partly a library problem.

The more familiar frameworks, examples, and structures I have available, the easier it is to produce a coherent answer quickly.

---

## Rules of Thumb

- Pause before speaking.
- Use a simple structure.
- Keep it short.
- Use experience I know.
- Avoid narrating uncertainty.
- End with a conclusion.
