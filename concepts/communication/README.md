# Communication

This section is my cross-course knowledge base for **designing messages that help an audience understand, remember, believe, and act**.

My working definition:

> **Communication is the deliberate design of attention, meaning, and action for a specific audience.**

# Cross-Cutting Ideas

## Audience Before Content

Communication quality depends on what the audience needs, not only on what the communicator knows.

## Intent Before Drafting

A message without a desired result becomes information transfer rather than strategic communication.

## One Big Idea

Complex supporting detail is easier to absorb when the audience understands the central point.

## Structure Is a Service

Visible structure reduces cognitive load.

## Clarity Is Not Simplification of Thought

Complex ideas can be communicated in simple language.

## Delivery Changes Meaning

Words, tone, pacing, expression, and physical behavior work together.

## Interaction Is Part of the Message

Questions, objections, and uncertainty are not outside the presentation. They are part of it.

## Communication Is Trainable

Video, feedback, rehearsal, and targeted behavioral goals make improvement concrete.
