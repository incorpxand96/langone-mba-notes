# Executive Email

## Definition

An effective business email is an asynchronous decision interface.

It should allow a reader to understand:

- why the message exists;
- what matters;
- what evidence supports it;
- what action is required.

---

## Framework

### Subject

Make it informative.

Use an action verb when action is needed.

### Opening

Get to the point.

State:

- recommendation;
- request;
- purpose;
- key benefit.

### Preview

For longer emails, tell the reader how the message is organized.

### Body

Use:

- short sections;
- headings;
- bullets;
- evidence;
- parallel structure.

### Close

Make the next step explicit.

---

## High Skim Value

A reader should be able to scan:

- subject line;
- opening;
- headings;
- bullets;
- closing

and still understand the message.

---

## How I Think About It

Email should be designed around the fact that the reader cannot interrupt the writer.

The draft should pre-answer:

- "Why are you emailing me?"
- "What do you need?"
- "Why should I agree?"
- "What happens next?"

---

## Rules of Thumb

- Specific subject line.
- Main point in opening.
- No buried request.
- Use headings for real structure.
- Keep lists parallel.
- Use white space.
- Be direct and courteous.
- Proofread.
- End with action.
