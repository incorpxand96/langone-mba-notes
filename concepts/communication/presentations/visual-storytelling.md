# Visual Storytelling

## Definition

Visual storytelling uses slide sequence, message titles, charts, images, and layout to reinforce the spoken argument.

---

## Three Steps

### 1. Storyboard

Decide the sequence of ideas before decorating slides.

### 2. Choose the Visual Form

Use:

- text;
- image;
- chart;
- diagram

based on the message.

### 3. Coordinate Design

Use:

- contrast;
- color;
- alignment;
- whitespace;
- readability;
- clarity.

---

## Message Titles

A strong title states the conclusion.

Weak:

> Sales

Strong:

> Sales hit a new peak in 2019

A reader should be able to skim titles and understand the story.

---

## One "So What?" Per Slide

A slide can contain multiple data points but should usually support one primary conclusion.

---

## How I Think About It

The most valuable slide element is often the title because it tells the audience how to interpret the evidence.

A chart without a message title forces the audience to perform the analysis during the presentation.

---

## Rules of Thumb

- Storyboard before formatting.
- One primary idea per slide.
- Use message titles.
- Make charts argue.
- Use images for concepts/emotion.
- Adapt density to presentation vs. preread.
- Never make the audience choose between reading and listening.
