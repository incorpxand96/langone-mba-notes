# Presentation Structure

## Definition

Presentation structure is the visible logic that helps an audience understand where the speaker is going and why.

---

## Reusable Structure

### Beginning

1. Grabber
2. Big Idea
3. Preview

### Middle

A small number of supporting points with:

- evidence;
- transitions;
- implications.

### Ending

1. Summary
2. Big Idea
3. Call to action / next step

---

## Why It Matters

The speaker has context the audience lacks.

Structure reduces cognitive load and protects attention.

---

## Chunking

Break the message into meaningful sections.

Use transitions to reset attention and make movement visible.

---

## How I Think About It

A presentation is easier to remember when the audience can reconstruct its logic.

The audience should be able to answer:

- What is the main point?
- What are the two or three reasons?
- What should happen next?

---

## Rules of Thumb

- Main point early.
- Preview the logic.
- Limit supporting points.
- Transition explicitly.
- Return to the Big Idea.
- End with action.
