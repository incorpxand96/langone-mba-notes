# Delivery and Presence

## Definition

Delivery is the visual, vocal, and verbal behavior through which a message reaches an audience.

Presence is the quality of being:

- credible;
- attentive;
- physically grounded;
- vocally intentional;
- responsive.

---

## Visual

- eye connection;
- facial expression;
- posture;
- gestures;
- movement.

## Vocal

- volume;
- pace;
- tone;
- enunciation;
- emphasis;
- inflection.

## Verbal

- words;
- fillers;
- sentence length;
- pauses.

---

## Congruence

Delivery should fit the content.

Confidence communicated verbally but contradicted by posture or tone weakens credibility.

---

## How I Think About It

Presence is not charisma theater.

It is communication discipline made visible.

A grounded speaker makes it easier for the audience to focus on the idea.

---

## Rules of Thumb

- Stable stance.
- Intentional eye connection.
- Conversational authenticity.
- Pause instead of filler.
- Emphasize key words.
- Move with purpose.
- Rehearse until flexible.
