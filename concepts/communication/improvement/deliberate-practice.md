# Deliberate Practice for Communication

## Definition

Deliberate practice improves communication by converting vague impressions into observable behaviors, then targeting a small number of those behaviors for change.

---

## Three-Step Review

### 1. Identify Strengths

Find specific things that worked.

### 2. Describe Problems Objectively

Describe behavior without self-judgment.

### 3. Choose Two or Three Improvement Goals

Pair each with a replacement behavior.

---

## Example

Vague:

> I looked nervous.

Behavioral:

> I kept my hands clasped for the first three minutes and spoke faster during the recommendation.

Replacement:

> Start with hands at a neutral resting position and pause before the recommendation.

---

## Why Video Helps

Memory is unreliable.

Video makes visible:

- fillers;
- pace;
- stance;
- eye contact;
- gestures;
- transitions;
- volume.

---

## How I Think About It

The useful unit of improvement is not:

- "confidence";
- "charisma";
- "executive presence"

in the abstract.

It is the observable behavior that produces those impressions.

---

## Rules of Thumb

- Start with strengths.
- Describe behavior, not identity.
- Use evidence.
- Fix a small number of things.
- Define replacement behavior.
- Rehearse.
- Review again.
