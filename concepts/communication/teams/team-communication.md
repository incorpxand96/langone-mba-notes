# Team Communication

## Definition

Team communication is the coordination required for several people to produce one coherent message and respond as one credible unit.

---

## Core Requirements

### Shared Strategy

Everyone understands:

- audience;
- intent;
- Big Idea.

### Role Coverage

Teams benefit from a mix of:

- action;
- social;
- analytical;
- creative

behaviors.

### Integrated Structure

Build the story before assigning parts.

### Transitions

Explain why one section leads to the next.

### Shared Q&A

All members should understand enough to contribute.

### Visible Attention

Non-speakers remain engaged.

---

## Trust

Trust makes it easier to:

- challenge ideas;
- delegate;
- recover from mistakes;
- share responsibility.

Without trust, communication friction becomes interpersonal.

---

## How I Think About It

The most common failure mode is parallel work disguised as teamwork.

Each person builds a section.

The deck is combined.

The message fragments.

True team communication requires shared ownership of the whole argument.

---

## Rules of Thumb

- Align on the Big Idea first.
- Assign roles after structure.
- Rehearse together.
- Plan handoffs.
- Use one visual system.
- Share Q&A.
- Keep non-speakers engaged.
- Build trust before demanding candor.
