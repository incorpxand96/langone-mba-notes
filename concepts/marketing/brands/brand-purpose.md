# Brand Purpose

## Definition

Brand purpose is the role a brand claims beyond the immediate functional transaction.

Purpose can range from communication to deep operating commitment.

## Four Levels

### 1. Purpose as Message

The brand expresses support.

### 2. Purpose as Positioning

The social or environmental idea becomes part of why customers choose the brand.

### 3. Purpose as Operating Model

The company changes product, supply chain, measurement, and investment.

### 4. Purpose as Ecosystem Intervention

The firm attempts to change behavior beyond its own products.

## Why It Matters

The deeper the purpose, the more credible, costly, differentiated, and difficult to imitate.

It also creates higher expectations.

## Example: Dove

Purpose becomes brand meaning.

The Campaign for Real Beauty redefines the emotional territory Dove wants to own.

## Example: Allbirds

Purpose changes materials, carbon measurement, innovation, suppliers, and partnerships.

Allbirds also shares tools and knowledge to influence the industry.

## How I Think About It

Purpose becomes strategically interesting when it changes decisions.

If purpose changes only the ad copy, it is fragile.

If it changes product and operating system, it can become a capability.

## Rules of Thumb

- Make purpose relevant to the business.
- Match rhetoric with operations.
- Expect scrutiny.
- Avoid purpose as costume.
- Give customers a way to participate when appropriate.
- Do not let purpose erase the product benefit.
