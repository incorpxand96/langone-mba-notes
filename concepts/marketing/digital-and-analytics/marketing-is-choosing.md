# Marketing Is Choosing

## Definition

Modern marketing offers more possible audiences, channels, formats, messages, moments, and metrics than any organization can optimize simultaneously.

Marketing therefore remains a discipline of **scarcity and choice**.

## Why It Matters

Digital can create the illusion that because an activity is technically possible and measurable, it deserves investment.

That creates content sprawl, platform FOMO, message dilution, attribution complexity, and organizational overload.

## Framework

Before adding a tactic, ask:

1. Which target?
2. Which journey stage?
3. Which behavior?
4. Why this channel?
5. Why this message?
6. What will we stop doing?
7. How will we measure incremental value?

## How I Think About It

More addressability increases the value of prioritization.

It does not eliminate the need for a coherent brand.

The future of marketing may involve more automated content generation. That makes human strategic choice more valuable, not less.

## Rules of Thumb

- Existence is not strategy.
- Every target costs attention and complexity.
- Every channel should have a role.
- Personalization should not destroy coherence.
- Ask what to stop.
- Optimize within a strategy, not instead of one.
