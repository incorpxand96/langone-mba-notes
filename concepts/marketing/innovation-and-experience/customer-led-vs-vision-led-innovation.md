# Customer-Led vs. Vision-Led Innovation

## Definition

Customer-led innovation begins with articulated or observable customer needs.

Vision-led innovation begins with a creator's belief that a new possibility should exist, even when customers cannot specify it in advance.

Both can create value.

## Why It Matters

"Listen to the customer" is correct but incomplete.

Customers are often good at describing current pain, friction, dissatisfaction, and desired improvement.

They may be worse at imagining new technologies, categories, or unfamiliar experiences.

## Framework

### Customer-Led Is Strong For

- usability;
- incremental improvements;
- unmet needs;
- feature prioritization;
- journey friction.

### Vision-Led Is Strong For

- category creation;
- radical experience;
- artistic innovation;
- technological discontinuity.

### Hybrid

Understand the customer deeply.

Then determine whether the solution should be requested, inferred, or invented.

## Example: Liquid Death

A low-cost marketing test provided evidence that the brand concept resonated before full scale.

## Example: elBulli

Ferran Adrià's process created experiences diners could not easily have requested.

Vision did not remove the customer. It changed the customer's role from co-designer to evaluator.

## How I Think About It

A useful question is:

> **Can the customer articulate the solution, or only the problem?**

Marketing research should discover both what customers say and what behavior reveals.

## Rules of Thumb

- Research problems before solutions.
- Use customers to validate assumptions.
- Do not outsource imagination.
- Prototype cheaply.
- Preserve operational feasibility.
- Distinguish novelty from value.
