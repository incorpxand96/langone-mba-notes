# Experiential Marketing

## Definition

Experiential marketing creates value or brand meaning through what customers **do and feel**, not only what they are told.

## Why It Matters

Experience can make intangible brand promises tangible.

A brand associated with adventure, luxury, craft, community, or discovery can create environments where customers live that meaning.

## Framework

Ask:

1. What should the customer feel?
2. What should they do?
3. Which brand association should the experience reinforce?
4. Where in the journey does it help?
5. Does it improve conversion, loyalty, or advocacy?

## Examples

### L.L. Bean

Outdoor activities turn retail into a demonstration of the brand's world.

### Allbirds Stores

Physical retail adds product trial, direct engagement, returns, and awareness to a brand that began DTC.

### elBulli

The journey, difficult access, kitchen tour, and multi-act meal become part of the product.

## How I Think About It

Experience is strongest when it is not an event bolted onto the brand.

It should be a **physical manifestation of the positioning**.

## Rules of Thumb

- Experience should reinforce positioning.
- Design for memory and behavior.
- Use physical channels for what digital cannot do as well.
- Use digital tools to reduce physical-friction gaps.
- Measure more than attendance.
