# Decision Biases

## Overconfidence

Confidence exceeds accuracy.

## Availability

Easy-to-recall information receives too much weight.

## Framing

Equivalent outcomes produce different choices depending on whether they are described as gains or losses.

## Sunk Cost / Escalation

Prior investment influences future investment even when future returns no longer justify it.

## Anchoring

Initial salient information pulls later estimates toward it.

## Countermeasures

- widen confidence intervals;
- seek base rates;
- reframe in multiple ways;
- ignore sunk costs;
- set exit rules in advance;
- generate estimates before seeing anchors.
