# Group Decision-Making

## Promise

Groups can outperform individuals by combining distributed knowledge.

## Failure Modes

### Groupthink

Consensus pressure suppresses dissent.

### Abilene Paradox

The group chooses something no one actually wants because everyone misreads everyone else.

### Group Polarization

Discussion makes the group more extreme.

## Process Design

- solicit independent views first;
- use a devil's advocate;
- ask for a second solution;
- record alternatives;
- protect dissent;
- manage time pressure;
- keep groups small enough for accountability;
- separate idea generation from commitment.

## Rule

> **The quality of a group decision depends on how information enters and survives the process.**
