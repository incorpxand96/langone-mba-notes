# Speaking Up and Dissent

## The Speaking-Up Dilemma

Speaking up can help the group while creating personal risk.

That makes silence rational in some systems.

## Leadership Responsibility

Leaders can reduce the cost of dissent by:

- inviting disagreement;
- avoiding punishment for bad news;
- separating challenge from disloyalty;
- asking lower-status members first;
- rewarding problem identification;
- protecting dissent before consensus.

## Everest Lesson

Strong hierarchy can improve coordination in dangerous settings.

But if hierarchy makes relevant information difficult to surface, it can also increase risk.

## Rule

> **Do not merely tell people to speak up; design conditions in which speaking up is rational.**
