# Leading Culture

## Definition

Culture is the shared pattern of values, beliefs, assumptions, and expected behavior in an organization.

## Levels

1. Artifacts
2. Espoused values
3. Underlying assumptions

## Espoused vs. Enacted

The strongest cultural signal is not what leaders say.

It is what the organization:

- rewards;
- promotes;
- tolerates;
- punishes.

## Formation

Culture is reinforced through:

- attraction;
- selection;
- attrition;
- socialization;
- rewards;
- role modeling.

## Rule

> **If enacted values contradict espoused values, employees learn the enacted values.**
