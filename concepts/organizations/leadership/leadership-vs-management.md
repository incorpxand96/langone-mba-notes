# Leadership vs. Management

## Management

Copes with complexity through:

- planning;
- staffing;
- organizing;
- control;
- problem solving.

## Leadership

Copes with change through:

- direction;
- alignment;
- motivation.

## Principle

Leadership and management are complements.

A strong organization needs both movement and operating discipline.

## Diagnostic Question

Does the situation lack:

- a destination?
- commitment?
- energy?
- structure?
- execution discipline?

Choose the intervention accordingly.
