# Vision

## Definition

Vision is a concrete description of a desirable future that guides present action.

## Effective Vision

A strong vision:

- evokes emotion;
- creates a mental image;
- connects future to daily work;
- gives a group a shared reference point;
- helps define success.

## Concrete vs. Specific

Specific goals can be abstract.

Visions often need imagery.

Numbers are useful for goals.

Pictures are useful for direction.

## Rule

> **Vision first; goals follow.**
