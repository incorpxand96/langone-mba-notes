# Leadership as Behavior

## Definition

Leadership is action that helps a group or organization move toward a better state.

It is not identical to:

- title;
- hierarchy;
- compensation;
- charisma.

## Core Implication

Instead of asking:

> Who is the leader?

ask:

> **What leadership behavior is required here?**

Possible behaviors include:

- creating direction;
- speaking up;
- mobilizing people;
- redesigning a system;
- creating safety for dissent;
- giving feedback;
- building a coalition.

## Romance of Leadership

Do not over-attribute outcomes to the visible person at the top.

Leadership matters.

So do:

- incentives;
- structure;
- culture;
- environment;
- group dynamics.

## Rules of Thumb

- Separate position from behavior.
- Diagnose before assigning blame.
- Lead from wherever relevant information exists.
- Judge effectiveness by outcomes and system effects, not symbolic authority.
