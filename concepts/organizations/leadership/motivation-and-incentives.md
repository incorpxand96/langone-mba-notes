# Motivation and Incentives

## Motivation

Motivation shapes:

- direction;
- effort;
- persistence.

## Sources

Extrinsic:

- pay;
- promotion;
- status.

Intrinsic:

- growth;
- challenge;
- meaning;
- autonomy;
- fairness;
- belonging.

## Expectancy

Motivation requires belief that:

- effort can produce performance;
- performance can produce outcomes;
- the outcome is valued.

## Equity

People compare contributions and rewards with others.

Fairness has motivational value independent of absolute pay.

## Incentive Design

Incentives communicate priorities.

Beware:

> **rewarding A while hoping for B.**

## Extrinsic Reward Risk

Performance pay can:

- undermine teamwork;
- shorten time horizons;
- encourage gaming;
- crowd out intrinsic motivation.

Use strongest where performance is objective and coordination needs are low.
