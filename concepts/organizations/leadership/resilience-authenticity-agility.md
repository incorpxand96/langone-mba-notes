# Resilience, Authenticity, and Agility

## Resilience Capabilities

### Emotional Intelligence

Regulate emotion and respond strategically.

### Authenticity

Create consistency between internal identity and external behavior.

### Agility

Adapt to barriers and convert them into new paths.

## Context Matters

Resilience is supported by:

- sponsors;
- candid feedback;
- opportunity;
- affirming environments.

## Rule

> **Do not romanticize resilience as purely individual toughness; context can either compound or reduce barriers.**
