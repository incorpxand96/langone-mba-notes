# Power and Influence

## Sources of Power

- authority;
- expertise;
- relationships;
- information;
- alternatives;
- scarce resources;
- reputation;
- coalition support.

## Dependence

Power is relational.

If another party depends heavily on something I control, my influence increases.

## Influence

Without direct authority, use:

- credibility;
- inquiry;
- framing;
- emotion;
- reason;
- common ground;
- coalition building.

## Rule

> **Formal power determines what I can order. Informal power often determines what actually happens.**
