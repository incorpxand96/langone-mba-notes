# Feedback as Learning

## Purpose

Feedback reduces the gap between:

- self-perception;
- external perception;
- expected performance;
- actual behavior.

## EOAC

### Expectations

What should good look like?

### Observations

What did I see or hear?

### Assessments

How does the behavior compare to the expectation?

### Consequences

What happened because of it?

## Principles

- ongoing feedback prevents surprise;
- process fairness matters;
- recipient perspective matters;
- describe behavior rather than identity;
- make next steps actionable.

## Rule

> **If the recipient cannot tell what behavior to repeat or change, the feedback is incomplete.**
