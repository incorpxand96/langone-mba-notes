# Social Dilemmas

## Definition

A social dilemma occurs when individual incentives conflict with collective interests.

## Examples

- free-rider / effort dilemma;
- status dilemma;
- individually based rewards for team work;
- speaking-up dilemma;
- short tenure vs. long-term group interest.

## Design Implication

Do not demand collective behavior while rewarding individual optimization.

## Rule

> **If cooperation repeatedly fails, inspect the incentive structure before diagnosing selfish personalities.**
