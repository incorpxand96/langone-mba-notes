# Task vs. Relationship Conflict

## Relationship Conflict

Personal and social tension.

Usually damages team functioning.

## Task Conflict

Disagreement about:

- ideas;
- analysis;
- work.

Moderate task conflict can improve performance, particularly on non-routine problems.

Too little can produce:

- apathy;
- weak challenge.

Too much can overwhelm coordination.

## Spillover Risk

Task conflict can become relationship conflict when people interpret disagreement as:

- disrespect;
- threat;
- identity judgment.

## Rule

> **Protect the right to challenge the work while protecting the dignity of the person.**
