# Congruence and Alignment

## Open-System Model

Inputs:

- environment;
- resources;
- history.

Strategy transforms those inputs through:

- work;
- people;
- formal organization;
- informal organization.

Outputs occur at:

- individual;
- group;
- organization.

## Principle

Performance depends on **fit** among the components.

There is no one best:

- structure;
- culture;
- people system.

There is only better or worse fit for the strategy and context.

## Diagnostic Use

When output misses the target:

1. identify the gap;
2. inspect the components;
3. locate misfit;
4. intervene at the leverage point;
5. observe second-order effects.
