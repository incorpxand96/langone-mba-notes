# Individual, Group, and System Analysis

## Three Levels

### Individual

- motivation;
- skill;
- bias;
- fatigue;
- emotion.

### Group

- norms;
- hierarchy;
- trust;
- coordination;
- decision process.

### System

- structure;
- incentives;
- resources;
- culture;
- environment.

## Principle

A problem can appear at one level and be caused at another.

The diagnostic task is to locate the real causal mechanism before choosing an intervention.
