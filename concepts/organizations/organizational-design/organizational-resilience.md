# Organizational Resilience

## Definition

Organizational resilience is the ability to continue functioning when:

- a key person leaves;
- information fails;
- demand changes;
- an assumption proves wrong;
- a shock occurs.

## Design Questions

- Where is knowledge concentrated?
- Which role is a single point of failure?
- Is succession credible?
- Can decisions be made without one individual?
- Are contingency plans explicit?
- Is communication redundant?

## Principle

> **A high performer can be an asset and a source of structural fragility at the same time.**
