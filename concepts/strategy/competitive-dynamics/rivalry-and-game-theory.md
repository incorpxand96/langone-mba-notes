# Rivalry and Game Theory

## Core Principle

Competitors react.

A strategy must therefore be evaluated as an interaction.

## Model

Specify:

- players;
- actions;
- timing;
- information;
- strategies;
- payoffs.

## Credibility

A rival threat matters only if execution is:

- feasible;
- economically rational;
- strategically worthwhile.

## Strategic Question

Do not ask only:

> What happens if we act?

Ask:

> **What will rivals do after we act, what will we do after that, and is the resulting outcome still attractive?**

## Principle

> **A competitive advantage that disappears after predictable rival response was never much of an advantage.**
