# Strategic Awareness in Oligopoly

Oligopolists should anticipate competitor reactions before acting.

## Direct Effect
What does the move do if rivals do nothing?

## Strategic Effect
What response does the move induce, and what is the resulting equilibrium?

## Scalability Risk

If multiple rivals can expand at low marginal cost and each believes it has an advantage, rivalry can become especially destructive.

## Principle

> **The relevant payoff is the post-reaction payoff, not the first-round payoff.**
