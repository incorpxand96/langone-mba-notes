# Market Structure and Concentration

## Useful Structures

- perfect competition;
- monopolistic competition;
- homogeneous oligopoly;
- differentiated oligopoly;
- winner-take-all;
- natural monopoly.

## Structural Drivers

Important determinants include:

- minimum efficient scale;
- network effects;
- differentiation opportunity;
- customer need structure;
- entry barriers.

## Concentration Is Ambiguous

High concentration can reflect:

- efficient scale/intangible investment;
- or barriers/rent seeking.

## Principle

> **Do not infer competitive health from concentration alone; explain why the concentration emerged and what happens to productivity, entry, prices, and investment.**
