# Positioning, Tradeoffs, and Fit

## Positioning

Choose a distinct way to serve customers.

Sources:

- variety;
- needs;
- access.

## Tradeoffs

A strategy becomes defensible partly because pursuing it requires not pursuing something else.

Tradeoffs can come from:

- brand/reputation;
- incompatible activities;
- different assets/skills;
- coordination limits.

## Fit

Activities reinforce one another.

Strong fit creates:

- better economics;
- consistency;
- imitation barriers.

## Operational Effectiveness

Operational effectiveness improves execution of activities.

It is necessary but generally insufficient for durable advantage because best practices spread.

## Principle

> **A strategy is a coherent system of choices whose tradeoffs and fit make profitable imitation difficult.**
