# Preemption and Profitable Imitation

A defensible position need not be impossible to copy.

It must be unattractive to copy **profitably**.

## Preemption Logic

The incumbent makes substantial investments that occupy the valuable segment.

A potential rival then faces:

- high required investment;
- small residual demand;
- uncertain customer switching.

## Defensibility Factors

- economies of scale;
- information frictions;
- experimentation risk;
- low share of buyer budget;
- continuous innovation requirements;
- network effects.

## Principle

> **The moat is the gap between what imitation costs and what the remaining market can support.**
