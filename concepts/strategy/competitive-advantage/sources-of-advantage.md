# Sources of Advantage

A useful taxonomy for the assets/capabilities behind WTP-C advantage:

## Relationship Capital
Hard-to-recreate relationships that change WTP, cost, access, or trust.

## Infrastructure
Physical/digital systems that lower cost, raise convenience, improve quality, or create entry barriers.

## Innovative Capacity
A repeatable system for generating and commercializing valuable change.

## Linchpin

One source is often the primary driver; the others reinforce it.

## Principle

> **Brand and market share are evidence. The strategic task is to identify the underlying investment that makes those outcomes possible.**
