# Scarcity, Scalability, and Scopability

A compact way to evaluate enterprise-value potential.

## Scarcity

Does the firm have defendable bargaining power rooted in a WTP or cost advantage?

## Scalability

Can the firm grow output while maintaining:

- margins;
- quality;
- unit economics?

## Scopability

Can the same advantage be monetized across new:

- products;
- geographies;
- customer groups;
- channels?

## Principle

> **Scarcity creates the profit pool, scalability expands it, and scopability redeploys it.**
