# Strategic Calibration: Weight and Variance

## Weight
How much does an attribute matter to WTP?

## Variance
How much do competitors differ on that attribute?

## Strategic Zone

The best differentiation opportunities tend to be attributes that are:

- high weight;
- high variance.

## Diminishing Returns

Once WTP for an attribute plateaus, incremental investment can shrink the WTP-C wedge.

## Principle

> **Do not maximize product attributes; calibrate investment to customer importance and competitive variance.**
