# WTP–Cost Wedge

## Formula

`Value Created = Willingness to Pay − Cost`

## Two Routes

Differentiation:

`WTP ↑`

Low cost:

`Cost ↓`

Both can expand the wedge.

## Critical Distinction

Raising WTP is not sufficient.

If:

`ΔCost > ΔWTP`

the strategic investment destroys value.

## Value Capture

The wedge can be distributed among:

- customer surplus;
- firm profit;
- suppliers;
- employees;
- intermediaries.

Industry structure affects who captures it.

## Principle

> **Competitive advantage requires superior value creation and the ability to capture a meaningful portion of it.**
