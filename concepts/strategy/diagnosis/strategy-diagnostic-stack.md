# Strategy Diagnostic Stack

Do not begin strategy analysis by choosing a favorite framework.

Begin with the performance problem.

## Level 1 — Industry

Use Five Forces.

Question:

> Is the profit pool itself structurally weak or changing?

## Level 2 — Position

Use WTP–C, scope, tradeoffs, and activities.

Question:

> Why does this firm perform differently from peers?

## Level 3 — Resources

Use RBV.

Question:

> What firm-specific assets/capabilities enable the position?

## Level 4 — Platform Dynamics

Use when value depends on interactions among user groups.

Question:

> Does participation itself change the value of the product?

## Level 5 — Rival Response

Use game theory / competitive dynamics.

Question:

> Will the economics survive rational competitor response?

## Principle

> **Framework choice should follow diagnosis, not habit.**
