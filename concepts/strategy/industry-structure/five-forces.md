# Five Forces

## Purpose

Explain the structural determinants of an industry's average profitability.

## Forces

- rivalry;
- buyer power;
- supplier power;
- threat of entry;
- threat of substitutes.

## Key Insight

The framework is about:

> **who has bargaining leverage over the value created in the system?**

## Important Rules

- One severe force can materially weaken industry economics.
- Do not average scores mechanically.
- Consider indirect customers and the entire value chain.
- Analyze change, not only current state.
- Use judgment and causal logic.

## Output

A Five Forces analysis should identify:

- where value is being competed away;
- which structural force is changing;
- what actions might reshape the structure.
