# Good Growth

Good growth raises enterprise value net of entry and ongoing execution costs.

## Four Mechanisms

Growth can create value by:

1. deepening/defending competitive advantage;
2. monetizing the core advantage more fully;
3. moving into a more attractive market;
4. improving fixed-asset utilization / unit cost.

## Rule of Thumb

Seek multiple mechanisms rather than relying on a single vague synergy.

## Principle

> **Growth is strategic only when the whole becomes more valuable than the pre-growth parts plus the cost of expansion.**
