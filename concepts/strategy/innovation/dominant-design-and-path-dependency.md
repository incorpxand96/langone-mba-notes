# Dominant Design and Path Dependency

A dominant design is an architecture around which customer demand and complementary investment converge.

## Positive Feedback

Adoption attracts:

- complements;
- suppliers;
- skills;
- infrastructure;
- customer familiarity.

These raise the design's value and increase further adoption.

## Principle

> **An early design win can become a durable ecosystem advantage when complements make the installed base increasingly valuable.**
