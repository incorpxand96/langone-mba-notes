# Incumbent Innovation Incentives

## Sunk-Cost Effect

Incumbents can rationally prefer to keep using already-funded assets rather than accelerate their depreciation.

## Replacement Effect

An entrant gains from becoming a competitor; an incumbent may merely replace one profitable position with another.

## Efficiency Effect

If credible entry threatens monopoly profit, preemptive innovation can be more valuable than accommodation.

## Principle

> **Whether an incumbent should disrupt itself depends on entry threat and incremental economics, not a generic command to innovate.**
