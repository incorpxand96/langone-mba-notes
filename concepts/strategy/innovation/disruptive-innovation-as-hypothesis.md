# Disruptive Innovation as a Hypothesis

Disruption is most useful when treated as a conditional theory rather than a synonym for successful innovation.

## Test

- low-end or new-market foothold?
- incumbent rationally ignores it?
- entrant improves toward mainstream needs?
- incumbent capabilities become liabilities?
- predictions fit the actual history?

## Principle

> **Use disruption to generate falsifiable questions, not as an after-the-fact label.**
