# Platform Business Models

## Definition

A platform creates value by facilitating exchanges among distinct participant groups.

## Strategic Questions

1. Who are the sides?
2. What transaction cost is reduced?
3. What rules govern exchange?
4. Which side creates value for the other?
5. Who should pay?
6. How does the platform capture value?

## Pricing

Platform pricing is structurally asymmetric.

Possible logic:

- subsidize price-sensitive users;
- subsidize marquee users;
- charge the side that benefits more from growth on the other side;
- use fees to screen quality.

## Viability

A platform is strategically useful only if it:

- meaningfully facilitates exchange;
- reduces real transaction costs;
- can monetize sustainably;
- resists disintermediation.

## Principle

> **Price the network, not each user group independently.**
