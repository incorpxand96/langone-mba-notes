# Allocentric Strategy

Allocentric strategy evaluates a decision from the perspectives of all participants whose behavior can affect firm value.

## Ask About

- customers;
- competitors;
- entrants;
- substitutes;
- complementors;
- suppliers;
- employees;
- regulators;
- acquisition counterparties.

## Direct vs. Strategic Effect

A decision's direct effect on the focal firm can be outweighed by the reaction it triggers elsewhere.

## Principle

> **Model other players' incentives and outside options before calling an action strategically attractive.**
