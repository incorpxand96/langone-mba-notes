# Ownership Advantage

Ownership advantage asks whether the **ultimate owner** improves the competitive or corporate advantage of the businesses owned.

Owners differ in:

- horizon;
- governance;
- risk tolerance;
- capital;
- incentives;
- stakeholder objectives.

## Three Levels

```text
Ownership advantage
      ↓
Corporate advantage
      ↓
Competitive advantage
```

## Principle

> **Ownership structure is itself a strategic choice, not merely a financing detail.**
