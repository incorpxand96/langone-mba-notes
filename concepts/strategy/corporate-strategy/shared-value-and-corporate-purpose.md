# Shared Value and Corporate Purpose

Shared value links social benefit and competitive/economic benefit.

Possible mechanisms:

- products and markets;
- value-chain productivity;
- local clusters.

Different owners can prefer different combinations of financial return, social outcome, and time horizon.

## Principle

> **A corporate-purpose claim becomes strategically meaningful when the ownership objective and the mechanism of value creation are explicit.**
