# Corporate Advantage

Corporate advantage exists when a corporate parent makes its businesses more valuable than they would be under feasible alternative ownership structures.

## Sources

- superior selection;
- restructuring;
- shared activities;
- skill/resource transfer;
- capital allocation;
- talent;
- governance;
- reputation.

## Costs

Subtract:

- HQ cost;
- coordination;
- bureaucracy;
- integration;
- loss of autonomy;
- entry premium.

## Principle

> **A collection of good businesses is not automatically a good corporation.**
