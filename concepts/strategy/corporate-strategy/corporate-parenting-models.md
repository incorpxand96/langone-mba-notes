# Corporate Parenting Models

## Financial Control

Low operating intervention; focus on targets, capital, leaders, returns.

## Strategic Planning

High involvement in business plans, interdependencies, initiatives, and shared capabilities.

## Strategic Control

Businesses develop plans; HQ challenges, approves, allocates, and intervenes selectively.

## Principle

> **The parent's degree of intervention should fit its actual parenting capability and the needs of the business.**
