# Divestitures and Refocusing

Exit is a corporate-strategy decision.

Potential reasons:

- a better owner exists;
- the business no longer fits parent capabilities;
- capital is better deployed elsewhere;
- coordination costs exceed synergies;
- standalone incentives or valuation improve.

Modes include sell-offs, carve-outs, spin-offs, and split-offs.

## Principle

> **A company should continuously re-earn the right to own each business.**
