# Three Tests of Corporate Strategy

## Better-Off Test

Does combining the businesses create more value than separation?

## Ownership Test

Is common ownership required, or could the value be achieved through an alliance, contract, JV, minority stake, or license?

## Organization Test

Can the owner design incentives, decision rights, processes, integration, culture, and resource allocation to realize the value?

## Principle

> **Strategic fit without ownership necessity and execution capability is not enough.**
