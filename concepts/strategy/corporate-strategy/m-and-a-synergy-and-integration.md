# M&A Synergy and Integration

## Synergy Types

- cost;
- revenue;
- capabilities;
- market power;
- capital/financing.

## Test Each Synergy

Ask:

1. What exact activity changes?
2. Is it technically feasible?
3. Does it require ownership?
4. What does it cost?
5. How long will it take?
6. What is the probability of realization?
7. Who captures it in the purchase price?

## Principle

> **Synergy becomes value only after operational execution and price discipline.**
