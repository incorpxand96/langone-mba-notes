# Portfolio and Capital Allocation

A multibusiness parent can create value by moving capital toward its highest-value use.

This requires:

- comparable opportunity evaluation;
- future-return estimates;
- willingness to reduce capital to mature units;
- avoidance of political budgeting;
- willingness to return capital externally.

## Principle

> **Internal capital markets are an advantage only when the parent allocates capital better than external markets and standalone boards would.**
