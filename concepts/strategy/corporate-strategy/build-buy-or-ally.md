# Build, Buy, or Ally

Changing scope requires a mode choice.

## Build

Best when capabilities can be developed internally, time is available, and integration risk outweighs speed.

## Buy

Best when control matters, resources are scarce, synergy is highly interdependent, speed matters, or the capability cannot easily be replicated.

## Ally

Best when uncertainty is high, resources are human/cultural, modular cooperation is possible, or flexibility is valuable.

## Principle

> **The right market does not imply the right ownership mode.**
