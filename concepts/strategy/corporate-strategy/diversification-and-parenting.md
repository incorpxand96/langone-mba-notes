# Diversification and Parenting

Diversification creates value only through a parent-specific mechanism.

Possible mechanisms:

- shared resources;
- skills;
- restructuring;
- internal capital allocation;
- reputation;
- governance.

Investor-level risk reduction is not enough because shareholders can diversify themselves.

## Principle

> **Diversification is valuable when the parent has a transferable parenting advantage—not merely because the portfolio contains different industries.**
