# Vertical Integration and Firm Boundaries

The boundary decision compares market contracting, quasi-integration, and common ownership.

## Integrate When

Ownership materially improves:

- coordination;
- protection of specific investments;
- reliability;
- market creation;
- resolution of hold-up/bargaining problems.

## Do Not Integrate Merely To

- smooth earnings;
- guarantee supply;
- capture an attractive supplier margin.

## Principle

> **Own the adjacent stage only when ownership solves a coordination or contracting problem more efficiently than the alternatives.**
