# Unbundling and Interaction Costs

Firm boundaries depend partly on whether coordination is cheaper inside the company or across market relationships.

Falling interaction costs can make specialization more efficient.

A useful distinction is among:

- customer-relationship businesses;
- product-innovation businesses;
- infrastructure businesses.

These activities can require different cultures and economics.

## Principle

> **Vertical integration that was once efficient can become obsolete when external coordination gets cheaper.**
