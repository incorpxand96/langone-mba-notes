# Family Enterprise

Family enterprise adds another system to ordinary business analysis: **the family itself**.

Ownership, control, management, family relationships, and wealth can interact in ways that create either unusually durable advantages or unusually difficult governance problems.

This branch will focus on:

- family ownership and value;
- governance;
- control rights;
- succession;
- family conflict;
- internal capital markets;
- liquidity vs. control;
- minority shareholders; and
- family offices.
