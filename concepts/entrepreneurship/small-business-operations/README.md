# Small-Business Operations

This branch focuses on the transition from a business that works because of individual effort to one that works because of an operating system.

Core themes include:

- owner dependence;
- key-person risk;
- management depth;
- SOPs;
- incentives;
- information systems;
- working capital;
- inventory; and
- professionalizing operations without destroying the strengths of an entrepreneurial organization.
