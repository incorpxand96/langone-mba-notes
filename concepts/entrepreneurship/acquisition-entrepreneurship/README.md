# Acquisition Entrepreneurship

Acquisition entrepreneurship is entrepreneurship through the purchase and operation of an existing business rather than the creation of a company from zero.

The central advantage is that the entrepreneur can acquire an existing operating machine:

- customers;
- employees;
- revenue;
- processes;
- reputation;
- supplier relationships; and
- cash flow.

The central risk is that the buyer can misunderstand that machine, overpay for it, finance it poorly, or damage the elements that made it work.

---

## Core Decision Sequence

1. **What kind of business should I search for?**
2. **Is this a good business?**
3. **Is it a good business for me to own?**
4. **What is sustainable earnings power?**
5. **What is it worth?**
6. **What can kill the thesis?**
7. **How should risk be allocated in the transaction?**
8. **What must be proven before closing?**
9. **What changes when the seller leaves and I take over?**
10. **How do I improve the company without breaking what already works?**
