# Cross-Course Connections

## Strategy I

Strategy I asks how a business creates competitive advantage.

Strategy II asks why that business should be owned by this parent alongside these other businesses.

## Finance

Corporate strategy depends on:

- opportunity cost of capital;
- acquisition premium;
- NPV;
- synergy valuation;
- capital allocation;
- ownership structure.

A strategic rationale does not rescue a negative-NPV deal.

## Accounting

Sum-of-parts and portfolio analysis depend on:

- consolidated statements;
- segment reporting;
- minority interests;
- parent debt;
- ROIC.

## Operations

Many synergies are operational claims:

- plants;
- procurement;
- logistics;
- systems;
- sales channels.

Modeled synergy must translate into process compatibility.

## Organizations

Corporate organization creates tensions between:

- autonomy and coordination;
- centralization and decentralization;
- local incentives and corporate synergy;
- culture preservation and integration.

## ETA / Search

The best-owner test maps directly into acquisition entrepreneurship:

> **Why am I a better owner of this business than the seller, another buyer, PE, or continued standalone ownership?**

## Family Business

Strategic ownership connects directly to family-controlled firms through:

- time horizon;
- control;
- reputation;
- succession;
- non-financial objectives.
