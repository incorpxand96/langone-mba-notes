# Corporate Headquarters and Parenting Advantage

The corporate center should exist because it adds value beyond its cost.

## Two Ways HQ Creates Value

### Selection
Choose what to own and when to buy/sell.

### Parenting
Improve businesses after ownership.

## Three Parenting Models

### Financial Control
Performance targets, capital allocation, management selection, autonomy.

### Strategic Planning
Deep HQ involvement in strategy, plans, interdependencies, initiatives.

### Strategic Control
Business units plan bottom-up; HQ challenges, approves, allocates, and intervenes selectively.

## Porter Mechanisms

- portfolio management;
- restructuring;
- transferring skills;
- sharing activities.

## HQ Can Destroy Value

- misleading guidance;
- bad performance targets;
- poor capital constraints;
- rigid policies;
- bad people decisions;
- misguided synergy programs;
- inefficient central services;
- time-wasting bureaucracy.

## Principle

> **A strong corporate center has a specific theory of how it improves businesses.**
