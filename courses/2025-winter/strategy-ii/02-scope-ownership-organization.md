# Scope, Ownership, and Organizational Design

Corporate strategy has three distinct dimensions.

## Scope
Where does the company participate?

- horizontal expansion;
- vertical integration;
- product diversification;
- geographic diversification;
- refocusing / contraction.

## Ownership
What relationship is required?

- wholly owned subsidiary;
- majority/minority ownership;
- joint venture;
- alliance;
- contract;
- licensing;
- standalone company.

## Organizational Design
How will ownership create value?

- centralization vs. decentralization;
- shared services;
- resource transfer;
- incentives;
- integration processes;
- capital allocation.

## Direction vs. Mode

**Direction** describes where scope changes.  
**Mode** describes how the change happens.

A decision can be right on direction but wrong on ownership mode.

## Three Tests

1. **Better-Off:** Is there value from combining?
2. **Ownership:** Does realizing it require common ownership?
3. **Organization:** Can the parent actually realize it?

## Principle

> **Should we be there, should we own it, and can we organize ownership better than anyone else?**
