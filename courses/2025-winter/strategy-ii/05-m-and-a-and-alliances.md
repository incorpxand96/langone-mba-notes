# M&A, Alliances, and Mode of Entry

Acquisition should be compared with internal development, non-equity alliances, equity alliances/JVs, and minority investment.

## M&A Motives

- overcapacity consolidation;
- geographic roll-up;
- product/market extension;
- acquisition as R&D;
- industry convergence;
- capability acquisition;
- economies of scale/scope;
- market power.

Different motives require different integration strategies.

## Synergies

### Cost
Plant consolidation, procurement, logistics, systems, overhead.

### Revenue
Cross-selling, route-to-market, geographic expansion, bundling.

Revenue synergies are usually more dependent on customers and competitive response.

## Build vs. Buy vs. Ally

Consider:

- synergy type;
- soft vs. hard resources;
- resource redundancy;
- market uncertainty;
- competition for scarce resources;
- need for control.

Broadly:
- modular synergies often fit non-equity alliances;
- reciprocal interdependence can favor acquisition;
- soft human/cultural resources make acquisition risky;
- high uncertainty can favor equity alliances.

## Value Capture

`Deal Value Creation ≠ Acquirer Value Capture`

The seller can capture much of the synergy through the acquisition premium.

## Principle

> **Why is acquisition the best governance mode for this relationship, at this price, given integration risk?**
