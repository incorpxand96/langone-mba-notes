# Corporate Purpose and Shared Value

The final session connects corporate strategy to shareholders, stakeholders, ESG, and ownership.

## Shared Value

Business can create economic value by addressing social problems through:

1. reconceiving products and markets;
2. redefining value-chain productivity;
3. enabling local cluster development.

## Shared Value vs. Philanthropy

Shared value is strongest when social benefit and business benefit are causally linked.

It is not simply charity or reputation spending.

## Competing Views of Purpose

One perspective emphasizes:

- shareholder accountability;
- competition;
- clarity of objective.

Another emphasizes:

- stakeholder dependencies;
- externalities;
- long-term social/business interdependence.

## Ownership Link

Different owners can prefer different mixes of:

- financial return;
- social outcome;
- time horizon.

## Principle

> **Corporate purpose becomes strategically useful when ownership preferences and the mechanism linking stakeholder benefit to business value are explicit.**
