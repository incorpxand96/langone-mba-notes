# Humana

Humana owned hospitals and managed-care health plans.

The strategic question was whether those businesses should remain integrated.

## Original Integration Logic

Managed care could:

- direct patients toward Humana hospitals;
- improve occupancy;
- coordinate cost;
- create overhead efficiencies.

## Changing Economics

Problems included:

- declining hospital occupancy;
- shorter stays;
- more outpatient treatment;
- reimbursement pressure;
- stronger HMO/PPO growth;
- rising administrative/medical costs;
- physician tensions.

## Spinoff Logic

Separation could create value through:

- clearer valuation;
- management focus;
- tailored incentives;
- independent capital allocation;
- separate acquisition strategies;
- removal of conflicts.

## Important Counterpoint

Kaiser showed integrated models could work.

Therefore Humana's problems do not prove integration itself was structurally wrong.

## Principle

> **A failed integrated company does not prove vertical integration is wrong; it may prove that this owner or organizational design is not the best way to realize the relationship.**
