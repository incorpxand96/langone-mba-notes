# Cadbury Schweppes and Adams

The case asks whether Cadbury Schweppes should bid more than $4 billion for Adams.

## Strategic Rationale

Potential benefits included:

- stronger position in global confectionery;
- entry into high-growth sugar-free gum;
- complementary geographic coverage;
- Adams route-to-market capability;
- procurement leverage;
- manufacturing/overhead rationalization.

## Revenue Synergies

- improve Adams marketing;
- cross-sell products;
- use Adams distribution;
- expand geographically.

These depend on customer/channel response and execution.

## Cost Synergies

- plant rationalization;
- shared purchasing;
- media buying;
- overhead;
- supply-chain changes.

But chocolate and gum have different manufacturing economics, so modeled savings are not automatically realizable.

## Competitive Pressure

A rival acquisition could strengthen Nestlé or another bidder.

But defensive logic does not justify paying more than Cadbury-specific value.

## Integration Risk

- leadership succession;
- organization design;
- systems;
- contracts/licensing;
- integration expense;
- optimistic base-case assumptions.

## Principle

> **Strategic logic is necessary but not sufficient: the board still needs a price ceiling based on buyer-specific synergy, integration cost, and execution probability.**
