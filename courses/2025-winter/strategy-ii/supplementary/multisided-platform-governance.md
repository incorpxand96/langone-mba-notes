# Multisided Platform Governance — Supplementary

This reading overlaps strongly with Strategy I's platform material, so it stays in the course folder rather than generating duplicate concept notes.

A multisided platform creates value by enabling direct interactions among multiple participant groups.

## Four Core Decisions

1. how many sides to bring onto the platform;
2. platform design;
3. pricing structure;
4. governance rules.

## Corporate-Strategy Link

The boundary question is central.

A firm may:

- produce complements internally;
- invite external complementors;
- resell;
- operate a platform.

These structures imply different ownership and control choices.

## Network Effects Are Not Enough

Cross-side network effects can increase value, but defensibility also depends on:

- switching costs;
- multi-homing;
- scale;
- governance;
- differentiation.

## Principle

> **A platform is an organizational choice about who participates, who owns what, who sets rules, and how value is divided.**
