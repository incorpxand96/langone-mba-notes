# My Cadbury / Adams Board Recommendation

Our group concluded that we would support a Cadbury Schweppes bid of roughly **$4 billion or slightly more**, but not materially higher.

## Reasons to Support

- sugar-free gum growth;
- Adams route-to-market capability;
- geographic complementarity;
- cross-selling;
- procurement leverage;
- potential supply-chain and overhead savings;
- Cadbury's acquisition/integration experience;
- competitive pressure from rival bidders.

## Reasons for Caution

### Manufacturing Compatibility
One of my strongest concerns was whether gum and chocolate production could actually share manufacturing assets efficiently.

### Management / Organization
Risks included CEO succession, Cadbury's decentralized structure, systems, and contract complexity.

### Forecast Optimism
The base case already required improvements; revenue synergy was even less controllable.

### Deal-Team Bias
A long internal pursuit can create sunk-cost bias and make walking away psychologically harder.

## Price Discipline

Our conclusion was not:

> Adams is strategically attractive, therefore bid whatever it takes.

It was:

> **Adams is attractive enough to support a bid around $4 billion, but execution risk and uncertain synergy should impose a ceiling.**

## Course-Discussion Insight

I specifically raised the difficulty of realizing cost synergies across different manufacturing processes.

That remains a durable lesson:

> **A spreadsheet synergy is not an operating synergy until the assets, processes, and incentives can actually be combined.**
