# Divestitures, Spinoffs, and Refocusing

Corporate strategy requires deliberate exit as well as entry.

## Why Separate Businesses?

- clearer strategic focus;
- tailored incentives;
- independent capital allocation;
- easier acquisitions;
- better owner;
- reduced complexity;
- removal of negative synergies.

## Costs of Separation

- lost operating synergies;
- shared customers/technology;
- tax/financing effects;
- reputation;
- separation costs.

## Modes

### Sell-Off
Business/assets sold to another owner.

### Equity Carve-Out
Minority stake IPO; parent usually retains control.

### Spin-Off
Shares distributed to existing parent shareholders.

### Split-Off
Parent shareholders exchange parent shares for separated-business shares.

### Tracking Stock
Economic tracking without true ownership separation.

## Principle

> **Divestiture is often the mechanism by which a company restores strategic coherence.**
