# Strategic Ownership and Ownership Advantage

The course adds a third level of strategy:

```text
Ultimate Owner
      ↓
Corporate Parent
      ↓
Operating Business
```

Corresponding advantages:

- ownership advantage;
- corporate advantage;
- competitive advantage.

## Who Sets Strategy?

A better answer than "the CEO" is often:

> **whoever has control.**

Control can sit with founders/families, managers, institutions, PE sponsors, activists, government, employees, or boards.

## Ownership Advantage

The extent to which the ultimate owner improves competitive or corporate advantage.

Owners differ in:

- horizon;
- risk tolerance;
- governance capability;
- liquidity needs;
- incentives;
- stakeholder objectives.

## Principle

> **Ownership structure is itself a strategic choice, not merely a financing detail.**
