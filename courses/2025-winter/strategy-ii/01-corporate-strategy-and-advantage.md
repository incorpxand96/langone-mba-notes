# Corporate Strategy and Corporate Advantage

Business strategy asks **how to compete in a business**. Corporate strategy asks **what businesses to own and how ownership creates additional value**.

## Corporate Advantage

Corporate advantage is the extent to which a parent enhances the competitive advantage or value of its component businesses over and above alternative ownership structures.

A business can have competitive advantage without its current parent having corporate advantage.

## Two Fundamental Sources of Corporate Value

### Selection
Choose what to enter, exit, buy, sell, and when.

### Parenting
Improve owned businesses through strategic guidance, capital allocation, talent, restructuring, sharing, reputation, or governance.

## Sum of the Parts

Standalone value is an important benchmark, but a holding-company discount or premium is not itself a complete diagnosis. Taxes, control, liquidity, debt, minority stakes, reporting, and market mispricing can matter.

## Net Corporate Advantage

Conceptually:

`Net Corporate Advantage = Value Created by Combination − Entry / Integration / Parenting Costs`

## Principle

> **Why is this business more valuable in our hands than in the hands of the next-best owner?**
