# Corporate Strategy Framework Cheat Sheet

## Fundamental Question

> **What businesses should we be in, who should own them, and how should they be organized?**

## Corporate Advantage

`Value under current parent > Value under next-best feasible owner`

## Three Dimensions

- Scope
- Ownership
- Organization

## Three Tests

- Better-Off
- Ownership
- Organization

## Vertical Integration

Compare:

- setup cost;
- transaction cost;
- transaction risk;
- coordination effectiveness.

## Diversification

Do not justify with shareholder risk reduction alone. Ask what parent-specific capability creates value.

## Build / Buy / Ally

Consider:

- synergy type;
- soft vs. hard resources;
- redundancy;
- uncertainty;
- competition for resources;
- integration capability.

## M&A

Conceptually:

`Buyer Value = Standalone Target Value + Buyer-Specific Synergy − Integration Cost − Premium`

## Divestiture Modes

- sell-off;
- carve-out;
- spin-off;
- split-off;
- tracking stock.

## Corporate Parenting

- financial control;
- strategic control;
- strategic planning.

## Ownership Strategy

```text
Ultimate Owner
      ↓
Corporate Parent
      ↓
Business
```
