# Trader Joe's

Trader Joe's is a strong positioning case because its advantage is difficult to explain through one isolated feature.

---

# Customer

The original ethos targeted:

- educated;
- sophisticated;
- adventurous;
- budget-conscious

customers.

---

# Activity System

Distinctive choices include:

- small stores;
- ~4,000 SKUs rather than tens of thousands;
- heavy private-label mix;
- direct purchasing;
- large purchases per SKU;
- no slotting fees;
- fast supplier payment;
- frequent product rotation;
- no conventional weekly sales;
- little traditional advertising;
- distinctive store culture;
- well-paid, highly trained generalist employees.

---

# Cost Side

Limited variety helps:

- purchasing leverage per SKU;
- supplier directness;
- inventory turns;
- store productivity;
- simpler operations.

---

# WTP Side

Trader Joe's raises WTP through:

- unique products;
- discovery;
- employee service;
- quirky brand;
- trust;
- shopping experience.

---

# Fit

The activities reinforce each other.

Example:

limited SKUs enable:

- scale purchases;
- private label;
- product knowledge;
- rapid rotation.

Employee investment supports:

- customer service;
- product sampling;
- store personality.

The system is the strategy.

---

# Financial Signature

The supermarket analysis shows how different strategies can reach good performance through different financial paths.

Trader Joe's model emphasizes both:

- strong asset/store productivity;
- differentiated customer value.

---

# Durable Lesson

> **A strategy is more defensible when the economics come from an interlocking activity system rather than one visible feature competitors can copy.**
