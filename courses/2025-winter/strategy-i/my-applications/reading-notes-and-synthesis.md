# My Reading Notes and Personal Synthesis

My personal notes covered the case packet and the major strategy readings.

The most useful themes are below.

---

# Strategy vs. Operational Effectiveness

Operational effectiveness is required but diffuses.

Strategy requires:

- distinctive activities;
- tradeoffs;
- fit.

The competitive advantage comes from the system.

---

# Financial Ratio Analysis as Strategy Evidence

The notes connect ROE to:

- margin;
- asset turnover;
- leverage.

For strategic comparison, leverage can obscure operating performance.

This reinforces the idea that financial statements can be used to **test** a strategy story.

---

# Resource-Based View

The readings broaden the classroom VRIO-style framework with:

- durability;
- appropriability;
- substitutability;
- competitive superiority.

This is useful because a scarce resource may still fail to generate shareholder value if:

- it depreciates quickly;
- another party captures the value;
- a substitute emerges.

---

# Platform Pricing

Platform pricing depends on:

- cross-side network effects;
- price sensitivity;
- quality sensitivity;
- marginal cost;
- same-side effects;
- marquee users.

Therefore "charge the customer" is often the wrong starting point.

The platform should price the system.

---

# Winner-Take-All

Tipping becomes more plausible when:

- network effects are strong;
- multi-homing is costly;
- differentiation needs are limited.

First mover helps, but the winner still needs a competitive advantage.

---

# Platform Threats

Important threats include:

- multi-homing;
- envelopment;
- discrimination by powerful intermediaries;
- disintermediation.

Strategies can include:

- supporting alternate platforms;
- building direct channels;
- exploiting a platform's need for completeness.

---

# Game Theory

A strategic interaction should specify:

- players;
- actions;
- timing;
- information;
- strategies;
- payoffs.

Then test the modeled result against business reality.

---

# Personal Synthesis

I think the strongest combined statement from these readings is:

> **Competitive advantage is a system-level outcome: industry structure determines the opportunity, positioning determines how value is created, resources enable the position, activity fit makes imitation difficult, and rival/platform dynamics determine whether the economics survive interaction.**
