# Impromptu Speaking

The course's key principle is:

> **Prepare for spontaneity.**

Impromptu speaking should not mean stream of consciousness.

---

# Preparation

Anticipate:

- context;
- likely topics;
- points I may want to make.

Then return to AIM:

- analyze the audience;
- identify the intent;
- stay focused on the message.

---

# In the Moment

## Pause

Do not begin speaking merely because silence exists.

## Relate to What I Know

Connect the question to:

- experience;
- facts;
- familiar concepts.

## Organize

Create a beginning, middle, and end.

## Begin and End Strongly

Give the listener structure quickly.

## Keep It Short

Impromptu communication often improves with compression.

## Speak Slowly and Conversationally

Speed can make unstructured thinking harder to follow.

---

# My Take

The paradox is that strong spontaneity comes from preparation.

The speaker who has:

- a clear message;
- familiar frameworks;
- practiced pauses;
- experience organizing thoughts quickly

can sound natural without sounding random.

---

# Rules of Thumb

- Pause first.
- Use AIM.
- Decide on two or three points.
- Do not narrate thinking process unnecessarily.
- Keep the answer short.
- Close with a conclusion.
- Use a conversational pace.
