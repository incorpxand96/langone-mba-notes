# Pricing

Price is the amount charged for a product or service.

Strategically, price also communicates value, quality, positioning, and category expectations.

## Price and Value

Value is the sum of benefits customers receive relative to alternatives.

Benefits can include speed, durability, convenience, less waste, easier setup, design, sustainability, and ecosystem value.

## Price Floor and Ceiling

### Price Floor

Product cost and required economics.

### Price Ceiling

Customer perception of value and willingness to pay.

### Between Them

Price is shaped by competitors, positioning, objectives, product line, and market conditions.

## Three Major Pricing Approaches

### Value-Based

Start from buyer perceptions of value.

### Cost-Based

Start from cost and desired profit.

### Competition-Based

Set relative to competitors.

A strong decision may use all three.

## Price and Positioning

Pricing should support brand strategy.

Premium, value, penetration, and product-line logic carry different implications.

A premium brand that prices like a commodity may weaken its meaning.

## Whole Product Value

For B2B technology, customers may value hardware, software, connectivity, support, pre-sales services, customer success, and ecosystem components together.

The customer buys the utility of the whole system.

## Breakeven and Profitability

Pricing must also account for uncertain volume, especially in new categories.

Consider unit economics, breakeven volume, operating-profit goals, price sensitivity, and competitive response.

## My Take

Price is both an economic variable and a brand message.

Optimizing price independently from positioning can change who buys, what the product signals, and the reference prices customers carry.

## Rules of Thumb

- Price relative to customer value, not just cost.
- Ensure price reinforces positioning.
- Consider product-line effects.
- Model breakeven.
- Do not train customers to wait for discounts accidentally.
- Remember that discounts change reference prices.
- Consider the value of the whole experience or ecosystem.
