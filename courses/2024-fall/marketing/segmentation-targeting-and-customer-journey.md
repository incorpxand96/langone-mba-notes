# Segmentation, Targeting, and Customer Journey

Segmentation decides how the market should be divided. Targeting decides which groups deserve distinct attention. The customer journey organizes what the brand should do as those people move from awareness to use, loyalty, and advocacy.

## Segmentation

Potential dimensions include demographic, psychographic, behavioral, life stage, journey stage, interest, frequency, and purchase behavior.

The purpose is not maximum detail. It is segmentation that leads to **different marketing decisions**.

## Personas

A useful persona can include:

- demographic context;
- goals and motivations;
- behaviors;
- category knowledge;
- decision criteria;
- barriers;
- channels;
- brands and influences.

Research methods include ethnography, interviews, focus groups, surveys, and empathy mapping.

## Microtargeting

Digital enables narrow targeting, which creates questions about how many personas to use, what variables matter, how much budget to allocate, which messages should change, which channels to use, and how to balance current vs. future users.

Over-fragmentation can dilute brand meaning, increase content burden, and make ROI harder to interpret.

## Customer Journey

The journey is the **entire experience across touchpoints**, not merely the purchase transaction.

Possible stages include awareness, familiarity, consideration, shopping, purchase, acquisition, ownership, retention, loyalty, repurchase, advocacy, and disposal.

## Journey-Based Marketing

### Awareness

Create relevance and notice.

### Evaluation

Provide evidence, differentiation, reviews, comparison, and product information.

### Trial / Purchase

Reduce friction and increase conversion.

### Retention

Create repeat behavior through quality, service, CRM, loyalty, and personalization.

### Advocacy

Let fans help market the brand through referrals, communities, sharing, and participation.

## Worked Example: Venture X

### Awareness

Search, YouTube, social, and travel-related targeting can reach affluent prospects before they are actively shopping for a card.

### Evaluation

Customers need annual-fee, reward, perk, lounge, travel-credit, and approval information.

### Purchase

The pre-approval tool reduces a major friction: customers can estimate approval likelihood without immediately accepting a hard credit inquiry.

### Existing Customer Growth

Capital One's owned data—spend, income, and existing products—can make cross-sell more efficient than cold acquisition.

## My Take

The journey is most useful when it prevents channel-first thinking.

Instead of:

> "We need TikTok."

ask:

> **"Which customer, at which stage, needs which behavior to change?"**

Then choose the channel.

## Rules of Thumb

- Segment only when it changes action.
- Avoid personas that are colorful but non-predictive.
- Match messaging to customer need, not merely demographic identity.
- Organize tactics by journey stage.
- Use owned data where it creates economic advantage.
- Treat retention and advocacy as marketing.
- Beware of microtargeting that fragments the core brand.
