---

# Shadow Side of Strengths

Strengths can become liabilities.

Examples:

### Self-Reliance

Useful:

- ownership;
- independence.

Risk:

- reluctance to depend on others;
- difficulty delegating.

### Achievement

Useful:

- effort;
- ambition.

Risk:

- over-optimization;
- crowding out recovery or relationships.

### Curiosity

Useful:

- learning;
- broad perspective.

Risk:

- overcommitment;
- fragmentation of attention.

### Fairness / Courtesy

Useful:

- supportiveness;
- trust.

Risk:

- over-accommodation;
- subordinating my own time too easily.

---

# Major Risks I Identified

## Overextension

Trying to do too much across:

- work;
- school;
- development;
- hobbies;
- relationships.

## External Dependence of Professional Outcomes

Promotion and role changes depend partly on:

- budget;
- organizational need;
- politics;
- timing.

## Relationship Underinvestment

Self-directed goals can crowd out relationships because the former are easier to schedule and measure.

## Insufficient Recovery

High activity without recovery can eventually reduce performance across every pillar.

---

# My Spring 2024 Insight

The key issue was not motivation scarcity.

It was **motivation competition**.

Many things mattered.

The leadership problem was resource allocation.
