# Feedback and Learning

Feedback is essential because self-assessment is imperfect.

There is often a gap between:

- how I see myself;
- how others see me.

Learning requires access to that gap.

---

# Why Feedback Fails

Common appraisal errors include:

- stereotyping;
- halo effect;
- recency bias;
- above-average bias;
- unskilled-and-unaware effects;
- self-serving bias.

People also differ in causal attribution.

Managers may explain weak performance through:

- ability;
- effort.

Employees may explain it through:

- insufficient support;
- context.

---

# Prevent Surprise

A core course rule:

> **Employees should never be surprised by formal feedback.**

Ongoing candid feedback helps align perceptions before the formal review.

It also improves procedural fairness.

---

# Procedural Fairness

People respond not only to the outcome of an evaluation but to the process.

Fair process can make an unfavorable result easier to accept.

---

# Start with the Recipient

Have the feedback recipient speak first.

This:

- gives voice;
- reveals self-perception;
- shows where perspectives align;
- identifies where the difficult discussion will be.

---

# EOAC Model

Developmental feedback can be structured as:

## Expectations

What does good look like?

## Observations

What did I actually see or hear?

Focus on behavior.

## Assessments

How does the observed behavior compare with the expectation?

## Consequences

What happened because of the behavior?

This avoids vague personality labels.

---

# Weak vs. Useful Feedback

Weak:

> "You are tough to work with."

Better:

- identify expected responsiveness;
- describe delayed or missing responses;
- assess the gap;
- explain the project and relationship consequences.

---

# Impression Management Principle

A memorable course line:

> **Be a critic to people's face and a champion behind their back.**

That combination can support honesty and trust.

---

# My Take

Feedback should reduce uncertainty.

If the recipient leaves only knowing that the manager is unhappy, the conversation failed.
