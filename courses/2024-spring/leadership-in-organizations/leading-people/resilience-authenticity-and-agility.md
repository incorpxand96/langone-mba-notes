# Resilience, Authenticity, and Agility

The *Beating the Odds* reading studies senior African-American women who reached high levels of leadership despite significant barriers.

Three capabilities stand out:

1. emotional intelligence;
2. authenticity;
3. agility.

---

# Emotional Intelligence

Includes the ability to:

- recognize emotion;
- regulate emotion;
- understand how others perceive behavior;
- respond strategically.

---

# Authenticity

Align:

- internal sense of self;
- outward expression.

The reading emphasizes:

- transparency;
- candor.

Authenticity does not mean expressing every thought or emotion without judgment.

---

# Agility

Agility means confronting obstacles and adapting effectively.

It includes the ability to transform barriers into new paths rather than simply absorb the setback.

---

# Relationships and Affirming Contexts

Individual resilience is not purely individual.

Leadership development depends on people and settings that:

- recognize talent;
- provide candid feedback;
- allow mistakes;
- create opportunity;
- remain invested in development.

---

# My Take

This reading complicates a heroic model of resilience.

People need agency.

They also need systems and relationships that provide opportunity.
