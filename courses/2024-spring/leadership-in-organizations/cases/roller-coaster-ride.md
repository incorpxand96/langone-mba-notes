# The Roller Coaster Ride

My case memo emphasizes the power relationships among Stephen, Peter, and Rina.

---

# Formal vs. Informal Power

Stephen has the strongest formal authority.

Peter has substantial informal power because he is:

- a star analyst;
- important to a major deal;
- difficult to replace.

Rina has less formal power but becomes strategically important because she is part of Stephen's contingency path.

---

# Trust

Trust is central.

Peter's decision process damages Stephen's confidence in the relationship.

This changes the value of a retention counteroffer.

Keeping a high performer is not automatically optimal when trust has collapsed.

---

# My Recommendation

My memo argues that Stephen should:

- allow Peter to leave;
- promote/develop Rina;
- recruit additional experienced talent.

The logic values organizational resilience over dependence on one star.

---

# Organizational Design

The case exposes deeper weaknesses:

- key-person dependence;
- weak succession;
- concentrated performance information;
- mismatch between talent-development rhetoric and operating mechanisms.

---

# My Take

The case changed the question from:

> "How do we retain our best person?"

to:

> **"Why is our organization so fragile that one person's exit is existential?"**

That is a much more useful management question.
