# Formula and Model Cheat Sheet

## National Income Accounting

`Y = C + I + G + X − M`

`NX = X − M`

`GNP = GDP + NFIA`

## Prices

`GDP Deflator = Nominal GDP / Real GDP`

Approximation:

`Nominal GDP Growth ≈ Real GDP Growth + Inflation`

## Growth

`Y = F(A, K, L, H)`

`Y = A × K^α × (HL)^(1−α)`

Simplified textbook form:

`Y = A × K^α × L^(1−α)`

TFP residual:

`A = Y / (K^α × L^(1−α))`

Steady state:

`Investment per worker = Depreciation per worker`

## Labor

`Unemployment Rate = Unemployed / Labor Force`

`Labor Force Participation = Labor Force / Working-Age Population`

`Employment-Population Ratio = Employment / Working-Age Population`

## Saving / External Balance

`S = Y − C − G`

`S = I + NX`

## Monetary Aggregates

`M = C + D`

`H = C + R`

`M/H = (1 + c) / (c + rr)`

## Real Interest Rate

Approximation:

`r ≈ i − Expected Inflation`

## Taylor Rule

`i_t = r* + π_t + a1(π_t − π*) + a2(log Y_t − log Y*)`

## Exchange Rates

Textbook convention:

`e = home-currency price of foreign currency`

`RER = e × P* / P`

## AS/AD Shock Signatures

### Negative Demand Shock
- `Y ↓`
- `P ↓`
- employment ↓
- unemployment ↑
- interest rates ↓

### Negative Supply Shock
- `Y ↓`
- `P ↑`
- employment ↓
- unemployment ↑
- interest rates ↑

## Policy Intuition

### Fiscal Expansion
Short run:
- AD ↑
- Y can ↑
- P ↑
- r ↑
- private C/I can be crowded out

### Monetary Tightening
Short run:
- rates ↑
- C/I ↓
- AD ↓
- Y ↓
- inflation pressure ↓
