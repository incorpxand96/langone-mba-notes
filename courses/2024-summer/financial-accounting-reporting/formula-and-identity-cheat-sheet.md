# Formula and Identity Cheat Sheet

## Fundamental Accounting Identity

`Assets = Liabilities + Equity`

`Equity = Assets − Liabilities`

## Net Income

`Net Income = Revenues − Expenses + Gains − Losses`

## Retained Earnings

`Beginning RE + Net Income − Dividends = Ending RE`

## Cash

`Beginning Cash + Net Cash Flow = Ending Cash`

Rearranged balance-sheet identity:

`Cash = − Non-Cash Assets + Liabilities + Equity`

## Receivables

`Beginning Receivables + Revenue − Receipts = Ending Receivables`

Therefore:

`Receipts = Revenue − Change in Receivables`

## Deferred Revenue

`Beginning Deferred Revenue + Receipts − Revenue = Ending Deferred Revenue`

Therefore:

`Receipts = Revenue + Change in Deferred Revenue`

## Payables / Accrued Expenses

`Beginning Payables + Expense − Payments = Ending Payables`

Therefore:

`Payments = Expense − Change in Payables`

## Prepayments

`Beginning Prepayments + Payments − Expense = Ending Prepayments`

## Operating Working Capital

`OWC = Current Operating Assets − Current Operating Liabilities`

## Net Operating Assets

`NOA = Operating Assets − Operating Liabilities`

## Invested Capital

`IC = Equity + Financial Liabilities − Financial Assets`

Conceptually:

`NOA = IC`

## ROIC

`ROIC = Operating Profit After Tax / Invested Capital`

## ROE

`ROE = Net Income / Equity`

Simplified leverage relation:

`ROE = ROIC + (ROIC − rd) × Debt/Equity`

## Turnover / Margin

`Invested Capital Turnover = Sales / Invested Capital`

`Operating Margin = EBIT / Sales`

`Pre-Tax ROIC = Operating Margin × Invested Capital Turnover`

## Time Value of Money

`FV = PV(1+r)^n`

`PV = FV/(1+r)^n`

## EAR

`EAR = (1 + APR/m)^m − 1`
