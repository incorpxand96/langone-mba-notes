# Dalio — How the Economic Machine Works

Ray Dalio's supplemental reading presents a transaction-based view of the economy.

## Core Framing

An economy is the aggregate of transactions.

A transaction consists of:

- buyer;
- seller;
- money or credit;
- a good, service, or financial asset.

Dalio emphasizes that spending can come from:

- money;
- credit.

Credit creates a claim on future money.

## Relevance to Finance

The reading helps connect financial markets to the real economy by emphasizing:

- credit creation;
- debt;
- asset prices;
- transaction flows.

It is best treated as supplementary framing rather than the core theoretical structure of the course.
