# Global Market Landscape — LSEG 2024

The LSEG supplemental report provides scale and context for the financial-market ecosystem discussed in class.

## Key Context

The report estimated total global financial-market notional outstanding at more than **$1 quadrillion** at year-end 2023.

Large categories included:

- rates;
- equities;
- foreign exchange;
- credit.

The value of this reading for the course is less about memorizing market-size numbers and more about seeing the enormous relative scale of:

- rates markets;
- derivatives;
- credit;
- equity markets.

## Course Connection

The course's market framework asks:

- who issues;
- who invests;
- what risks are transferred;
- how price is determined;
- what institutions enable the transaction.

The LSEG report gives empirical scale to that framework.
