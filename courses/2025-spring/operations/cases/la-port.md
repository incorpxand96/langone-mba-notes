# LA Port Bottleneck

The mini-case tracks containers through:

1. docking;
2. unloading;
3. trucking;
4. warehouse release.

Supplied capacities are approximately:

- docking: 100 containers/hour;
- unloading: 50 containers/hour;
- trucks: 40 containers/hour;
- warehouses: 10 containers/hour.

Therefore:

`System Capacity = 10 containers/hour`

Warehouses are the bottleneck.

---

# Why Ships Queue Offshore

The queue appears far upstream.

Warehouse congestion causes:

- containers to accumulate;
- unloading to slow;
- docks to fill;
- ships to wait.

Thus the visible symptom is not the true bottleneck.

---

# Managerial Lesson

Adding:

- docks;
- cranes;
- trucks

without increasing warehouse release does not improve total throughput.

It merely changes where inventory waits.

---

# Durable Lesson

> **Trace the whole process before investing in the place where the queue is visible.**
