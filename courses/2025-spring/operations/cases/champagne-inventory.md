# Champagne Inventory

The case examines inventory policy for high-value Champagne SKUs at a Manhattan liquor store.

Operational complications include:

- limited shelf/storage space;
- costly climate-controlled storage;
- customer service requirements;
- weekly ordering;
- many SKUs.

---

# Holding Cost

Storage cost includes not only physical storage expense but also:

> **opportunity cost of space**

A bottle occupying constrained storage prevents another item from using that resource.

---

# Stockout Cost

The store also incurs customer-service costs when inventory is unavailable.

Its customer promise includes a waiting discount, making shortage costs observable.

---

# Core Tradeoff

More inventory:

- improves availability;
- raises holding cost.

Less inventory:

- reduces holding cost;
- increases shortage/customer cost.

This is the basic economic tension behind inventory management.

---

# Durable Lesson

> **Inventory policy should price both the cost of excess and the cost of absence.**
