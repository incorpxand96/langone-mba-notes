# Benihana

## Core Question

How can a restaurant's operating process create both a distinctive customer experience and superior economics?

---

# Product Strategy

Benihana's product can be described through:

- moderate cost;
- low food variety;
- relatively quick meal;
- high experiential/service quality.

The hibachi chef's showmanship makes the process part of the product.

---

# Operational Design

Key choices include:

- very limited menu;
- standardized ingredients;
- communal table seating;
- batching customers;
- chef multitasking;
- controlled meal pacing;
- minimal back-of-house space;
- limited pre-cooking.

---

# Cost Effects

The case notes attribute stronger economics partly to:

- reduced food waste;
- easier demand prediction;
- volume purchasing;
- high chef productivity;
- better space utilization.

Only a relatively small share of the restaurant is devoted to non-revenue-generating space.

---

# Customer Flow

Batching customers at tables has multiple effects:

- improves chef utilization;
- increases bar waiting time/revenue;
- creates a group entertainment experience.

The same operational choice affects both:

- process economics;
- customer value proposition.

---

# Strategic Fit

The case illustrates the course's strategic audit:

1. define product attributes;
2. define operating objectives;
3. examine resources/processes;
4. test fit;
5. connect operating choices to profitability.

---

# Durable Lesson

> **The best operating choices can improve cost and strengthen the product at the same time.**
