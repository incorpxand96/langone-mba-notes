# Final Review Map

The supplied final-preparation guide emphasizes seven areas.

## 1. Process Analysis

Know:

- inputs;
- outputs;
- flow units;
- resources;
- process-flow diagrams;
- throughput;
- flow time;
- inventory;
- Little's Law;
- capacity;
- utilization;
- bottlenecks.

## 2. Working Capital

Know:

- DIO;
- DSO;
- DPO;
- CCC;
- interpretation of the cash cycle.

## 3. Optimization

Know how to read:

- Answer Report;
- Sensitivity Report;
- binding vs. non-binding constraints;
- slack;
- shadow prices;
- allowable ranges.

## 4. Variability and Queues

Know:

- arrival rate;
- service rate;
- coefficient of variation;
- utilization;
- waiting time;
- average queue length;
- M/M/s spreadsheet interpretation.

## 5. Inventory

Know:

- underage cost;
- overage cost;
- critical fractile;
- normal-demand optimal quantity.

## 6. Supply Chain

Know bullwhip causes:

- forecast updating;
- batching;
- price fluctuation;
- rationing.

Know mitigations:

- information sharing;
- smaller/more frequent orders;
- stable pricing;
- better allocation rules.

## 7. Cases

Be able to analyze operational capabilities in:

- Zara;
- Apple.

The broader cases also reinforce:

- strategic fit — Benihana;
- bottlenecks — LA Port;
- inventory economics — Champagne;
- digital capacity — OpenAI.
