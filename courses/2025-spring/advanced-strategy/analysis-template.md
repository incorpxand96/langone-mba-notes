# Advanced Strategy Analysis Template

Use this sequence to analyze a firm.

## 1. Define the Industry and Value Chain

- What industry/stage is the focal firm in?
- Who supplies it?
- Who buys from it?
- What substitutes/complements matter?
- What market structure describes the stage?

## 2. Test Industry Attractiveness

Use Five Forces without confusing the industry with the focal company.

Ask where profit is likely to persist in the value chain.

## 3. Define the Trading Partners' Outside Options

For each important participant:

- what is their WTP or opportunity cost?
- what alternatives do they have?
- why do they prefer the focal firm?

## 4. Identify the WTP-C Advantage

What specific customer/supplier outcome is better?

Avoid vague answers such as "brand" or "quality."

## 5. Calibrate Attributes

For key attributes:

- weight;
- variance;
- current investment level;
- point of diminishing returns.

## 6. Identify the Linchpin Investment

Is the primary source:

- relationship capital;
- infrastructure;
- innovative capacity?

How do the other two reinforce it?

## 7. Test Defensibility

Can a rival profitably copy the position?

Consider:

- scale requirement;
- information frictions;
- experimentation risk;
- budget share;
- innovation requirements;
- network effects;
- residual market size.

## 8. Look for Strategic Effects

What reactions will come from:

- rivals;
- suppliers;
- customers;
- entrants;
- substitutes;
- complementors;
- regulators?

## 9. Validate Financially

Inspect:

- ROIC vs. WACC;
- economic profit;
- margin drivers;
- asset turnover;
- capital intensity;
- relative cost structure.

## 10. Evaluate Growth

Does growth:

- deepen/defend advantage?
- monetize the core?
- improve market attractiveness?
- improve asset utilization?

## 11. Conclude With the Three S's

### Scarcity
How strong/defensible is the advantage?

### Scalability
Can volume grow without degrading economics?

### Scopability
Can the advantage travel?
