# Strategy Memo Narrative Template

The sample Sherwin-Williams narrative suggests a strong strategy memo should tell a causal story rather than list frameworks.

## Opening Thesis

State:

1. what makes the firm added value;
2. which investment is the linchpin;
3. why the position is sustainable;
4. what the financial evidence shows.

## Suggested Flow

### 1. Market / Value-Chain Context
Define the stage and why it is attractive or difficult.

### 2. Added-Value Proposition
Explain the firm's WTP-C advantage.

### 3. Linchpin Asset / Capability
Choose among:

- relationship capital;
- infrastructure;
- innovative capacity.

### 4. Reinforcing Investments
Show how the other two categories amplify the linchpin.

### 5. Defensibility
Explain why imitation is uneconomic.

### 6. Financial Footprint
Tie the claim to:

- ROIC;
- margins;
- asset turns;
- relative cost structure.

### 7. Growth
Evaluate growth through the four good-growth tests.

### 8. Risk
Identify changes in:

- taste;
- technology;
- regulation;
- global trade;
- capital markets;
- ecosystem relationships.

## Writing Rule

Do not write:

> "The company has a strong brand and strong management."

Write:

> **The company invested in X, which changes Y for a specific trading partner, raising WTP/lowering cost by mechanism Z; the investment is difficult to copy because ___, and the resulting advantage is visible in ___.**
