# Cross-Course Connections

## Strategy I

Strategy I introduced:

- Five Forces;
- WTP-C;
- positioning;
- resources;
- rivalry.

Advanced Strategy makes these more economic and testable through:

- added value;
- bargaining;
- ROIC/WACC;
- profitable imitation;
- weight/variance.

## Strategy II / Corporate Strategy

Good Growth deepens the corporate-strategy question.

Growth in scope should create value through identifiable mechanisms rather than generic synergy.

The managerial-kiss warning also reinforces the best-owner test.

## Finance

The course links strategy directly to:

- ROIC;
- WACC;
- economic profit;
- enterprise value;
- real options;
- capital allocation.

## Accounting

Financial statements provide the footprint of advantage through:

- gross margin;
- SG&A;
- working capital;
- asset turns;
- invested capital.

## Operations

Infrastructure advantage often comes from:

- distribution;
- production;
- logistics;
- utilization;
- fixed-cost amortization.

## Organizations

Innovative capacity and relationship capital depend on:

- culture;
- incentives;
- organizational design;
- talent systems;
- trust.

Disney is the clearest example.

## Entrepreneurship / ETA

The framework is highly portable to target evaluation:

1. Is the target genuinely added value?
2. What creates its scarcity?
3. Can competitors copy it profitably?
4. Is it scalable?
5. Is it scopable?
6. Does ROIC/economic profit support the story?

This is a strong complement to an ETA moat analysis.
