# Oligopoly, Rivalry, and Strategic Awareness

A concentrated market can still produce very different profit outcomes depending on how rivals behave and how scalable they are.

## Four Duopoly Patterns

### Collusion
Firms coordinate toward monopoly-like price and quantity.

### Strategic Awareness
Firms do not collude but anticipate each other's reactions and avoid some mutually destructive moves.

### First-Mover Advantage
One firm establishes an advantageous position before the other.

### Scalable Competition
Both firms believe they are advantaged and can expand cheaply, producing aggressive price/feature competition.

## Industry Profit Implication

Holding other conditions constant, the course ranks total industry profit roughly:

`Collusion > Strategic Awareness > First-Mover > Scalable Competition`

## Why Scalability Can Be Dangerous

Scalability is wonderful for one advantaged firm.

It can be destructive when several rivals can expand rapidly and each expects to win.

## Strategic Effects

Always ask:

- What happens directly to us?
- How will the rival respond?
- What happens after the response?
- Does the equilibrium still improve our economics?

## Principle

> **In oligopoly, the value of an action depends on the reaction it induces.**
