# Domino's

Domino's illustrates how infrastructure and organizational design can support a differentiated delivery proposition at large scale.

## Strategic Investments

Potential sources of advantage include:

- ordering technology;
- delivery systems;
- store network;
- franchise training/support;
- standardized processes;
- brand and promotions.

## Franchise Model

Domino's receives royalties while franchisees supply much of the local operating capital.

A notable feature is that a very high share of franchisees come from inside the Domino's system.

That can reduce information and training risk because prospective owners already understand:

- operating routines;
- software;
- delivery system;
- brand standards.

## Relationship Capital

The franchisor-franchisee relationship matters because both sides need positive economics.

An extractive royalty model that destroys franchisee returns would undermine the system.

## Infrastructure as a Flywheel

Digital ordering and delivery infrastructure can:

- raise customer convenience/WTP;
- increase order volume;
- improve data;
- support franchisee economics;
- increase system scale.

## Principle

> **A scalable network is strongest when the economics remain attractive to the local operators who actually deliver the customer experience.**
