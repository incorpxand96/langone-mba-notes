# Sources of Advantage

Marciano groups the assets and capabilities behind WTP-C advantage into three broad buckets.

## 1. Relationship Capital

Value embedded in important connections with:

- customers;
- suppliers;
- employees;
- distributors;
- governments/institutions;
- other strategic partners.

Relationship advantage is strongest when relationships are hard to recreate and materially improve WTP or cost.

It is not simply "we have lots of customers."

## 2. Infrastructure

Physical or digital assets such as:

- plants;
- stores;
- distribution;
- logistics;
- equipment;
- IT systems;
- delivery networks.

Infrastructure can:

- reduce cost;
- improve convenience/service;
- raise WTP;
- deter entry.

## 3. Innovative Capacity

The ability to repeatedly generate and commercialize improvements.

Important when products or processes have short shelf lives.

Innovative capacity can depend on:

- leadership;
- incentives;
- organizational design;
- supplier/customer collaboration;
- talent;
- geographic/product scope that monetizes innovation.

## Linchpin Logic

Usually one category is the primary source of advantage.

The other two reinforce it.

Think of the three as a flywheel rather than three independent checkboxes.

## Evidence vs. Cause

Potential evidence:

- brand equity;
- market share;
- margins;
- ROIC.

Potential causes:

- relationship capital;
- infrastructure;
- innovative capacity.

## Principle

> **Do not confuse the observed success of a business with the asset or capability that produced it.**
